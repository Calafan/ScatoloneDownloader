# Mill

Canonical record of the rulings for `CardEffect.Mill`. The one-line tooltip shown in the tagger is
in [`EffectGlossary.cs`](../../ScatoloneDownloader/Mtg/EffectGlossary.cs).

**Definition, 2026-11-10** ("Sì, così"; Glimpse the Unthinkable is the card): ANOTHER player loses
cards off the TOP of their library — or the BOTTOM, see the end — to your card, put into their
graveyard, or exiled when the card plays none of them. "Target player" counts, and so does "each player" (13 of 13 reviewed for
"mills"), and so do rad counters handed to a player (16 of 16). Out: your own library, which is
fuel (0 of 7 reviewed "you mill"), and the top exiled for cards you then play, which is
CardAdvantage at most (0 of 25 reviewed, 21 of them in the sitting of 2026-11-10). Every ruling
below is consistent with it; the two exceptions it keeps are a top the player keeps part of (Q3) and
the top exiled to be played. Read with it the same day: "puts all cards revealed this way into their
graveyard" (Mind Funeral, Mind Grind), "all noncreature cards revealed this way" (Telemin
Performance), "then puts those cards into their graveyard" (Balustrade Spy, Mirko Vosk, Undercity
Informer, Trepanation Blade, Destroy the Evidence's "its controller"), and "each player exiles the
top" with nothing played (Timesifter) — all unreviewed, none read before.

put cards from a library into a graveyard (Glimpse the Unthinkable, Millstone, Altar of Dementia).
Only when it is aimed at SOMEBODY ELSE. A bare "mill three cards" fills your own graveyard, which is
fuel for what the card does next rather than an attack on anybody — Stinkweed Imp's dredge and Fell
Gravship's self-mill are graveyard cards, not mill cards.

Narrowed 2026-09-15; the older cost exclusion (Deep Spawn, Millikin) follows from the same reading,
since a price is always paid by yourself.

The top of their library EXILED is the same attack, read 2026-10-15 from the three reviewed cards
that say it (Ulamog, the Defiler; Ashiok, Wicked Manipulator; Malboro) — unless the exiled cards are
then played, which was Steal until 2026-11-08 and is CardAdvantage at most since (Outrageous Robbery,
Bloodsoaked Insight, Crabomination).

RAD COUNTERS handed to a player are Mill too, "each player" included (13 of 13 reviewed), but not
the ones you get.

Read 2026-11-06: "exiles CARDS FROM the top of their library until they exile a land card" is the
same attack as "exiles the top three cards" (Umbris, Fear Manifest, 1 of 1; Tasha's Hideous Laughter
is proposed the same, and the five reviewed cards that say it and then cast the card stay Steal or
Removal; no Steal since 2026-11-08). And "for each opponent, YOU mill a card" mills you, not them (Sivriss, Nightmare Speaker,
untagged, 0 of 1).

RULED 2026-11-06, "Sì, entrambe Mill" (Curse of Unbinding, Mill by hand, against Sméagol, Helpful
Guide, Steal alone until 2026-11-08): an OPPONENT who reveals cards from the top of their library until a card of
some kind, that card taken (to your battlefield, your hand, wherever) and the REST of the revealed
cards put into their graveyard, is Mill, in both wordings: "That player puts the rest of the
revealed cards into their graveyard" and "and the rest into their graveyard". Another player's
library only: your own library revealed into your own graveyard is another question (0 reviewed
cards say it).

RULED 2026-11-10, Q1 "Mill": a top LOOKED AT or revealed, and the cards for the graveyard CHOSEN by
you, is Mill — a card chosen out of their top is still a card they lose, as Ray of Erasure's blind
one is. Cruel Fate (no tag since Filter's Q6 of 2026-11-06), Eye Spy and Wand of Denial (Filter,
"target player") +Mill, named; 0 of 3 reviewed before, the question had never been asked. Wu Spy,
Dimir Charm, Lurking Informant, Draugr Thought-Thief, Thief of Sanity, Bamboozle, Balshan Beguiler
and Dakra Mystic follow, unreviewed.

RULED 2026-11-10, Q2 "Conta, come Burn": a TOKEN's quoted ability that exiles their top counts, as
Burn's Q6 of 2026-11-06 counts a token's. The veto written for Ashiok, Nightmare Muse is gone; it
was the one card, unreviewed, and the "mills" rule never had it.

RULED 2026-11-10, Q3 "Fuori": a player who reveals their top and KEEPS part of it — the card they
named into their hand (Petra Sphinx, untagged; Vexing Arcanix, Burn), or the revealed permanents
onto their own battlefield (Over the Top, CardAdvantage) — is not Mill, 0 of 3 reviewed: the card
hands them something, so it is no attack on their library. Guild Feud and Pyxis of Pandemonium
follow, unreviewed. The card taken "under your control" is yours, not theirs, so Curse of Unbinding,
Sméagol and Telemin Performance stay Mill.

RULED 2026-11-10, "Cellar Door è in effetti macina": the BOTTOM card of their library put into their
graveyard is the same attack as the top ("Target player puts the bottom card of their library into
their graveyard"). Cellar Door is the one card that says it, unreviewed; 0 reviewed. A library
SEARCHED into their graveyard (Life's Finale, Quiet Speculation, unreviewed) was not asked and stays
out.

RULED 2026-11-12, "Almeno 3 o ripetuto": a mill made ONCE has to take three cards or more; one or
two cards once is a rider beside what the card is for, as a single scry is for Filter. A REPEATED
mill counts from one card: a trigger, an activation (one the card grants in quotes too, Stern
Mentor), a loyalty ability, a saga chapter that comes twice ("I, II —", Death in Heaven), rad
counters. So does an amount that is not fixed: X, "that many", "equal to", "for each" (Sanity
Grinding), half a library. Read one by one on the 121 reviewed Mill cards: 15 take one or two cards
once and are reached and handed back (Atrocious Experiment, Cruel Fate, Demogorgon's Clutches,
Desperate Bloodseeker, Disturbing Conversion, Draugr Thought-Thief, Eye Spy, Flitting Guerrilla,
Foreshadow, Gnawing Vermin, Halo-Charged Skaab, Overwhelmed Apprentice, Path of the Schemer, Ray of
Erasure, Watcher of the Wayside), and 14 take three once and keep it (Drownyard Amalgam, Deal Gone
Bad). Of the cards the 11-10 look-at reading reached, Wu Spy and Dimir Charm put two cards into the
graveyard once and lose it, unreviewed, with Thought Scour, Pilfered Plans, Psychic Strike and 11
more.
