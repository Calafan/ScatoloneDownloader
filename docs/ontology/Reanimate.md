# Reanimate

Canonical record of the rulings for `CardEffect.Reanimate`. The one-line tooltip shown in the tagger
is in [`EffectGlossary.cs`](../../ScatoloneDownloader/Mtg/EffectGlossary.cs).

ANOTHER nonland PERMANENT comes back FROM THE DEAD onto the battlefield under YOUR control without
its mana cost paid: out of a graveyard, or out of the exile this card put it in from a graveyard or
as it died. Reanimate is the card. It counts with a real alternative cost, for one turn only, and as
a token copy, and under any mana-value cap — Cheat refuses a cap, this tag does not (62 of 62 capped
returns tagged by hand). The rest follows from the sentence: a land is not this tag (nonland), nor a
card bringing itself back (another), nor one handed to its owner or an opponent (your control), nor
a blink or a card off a library (from the dead), nor a cast that pays the cost (Noctis). The one
exception kept, with its why, is the creature TYPE (ruled 2026-10-15): a return only a tribe can use
needs a tribal deck. Redefined 2026-11-06, the last rulings below; every earlier paragraph marked
"overturned" is history, and no card is kept in or out of this tag by name.

Three more routes ruled 2026-09-19, because the graveyard is not the only place one comes back from
and the tag was missing a third of what it should hold. A card THIS CARD EXILED, put onto the
battlefield under your control, is the same trick with a waiting room in the middle (Purgatory,
Sothera — OVERTURNED 2026-11-06, exiled off the battlefield, Steal —, The Darkness Crystal, Ghost
Vacuum) — but blinking a creature YOU CONTROL is not, because
Cold Storage and Safe Haven exile your own and hand it back, which is a strange kind of protection
and stays untagged.

A TOKEN COPY of a creature card in a graveyard is not the card itself, but what lands on the table
is the same thing (Cursecloth Wrappings' embalm, Ardyn, Coiling Rebirth). And a LAND out of the
graveyard is not this tag (7 reviewed cards put one back and only 1 carried it) — nor Ramp, measured
2026-09-29 at 1 of 8 (Lumra the one): the human files it under Regrowth or nothing (Undergrowth
Recon was handed back without the Regrowth tag on 2026-10-16; Ramp only if the card filled the
graveyard itself, ruled 2026-10-13, Ramp.md). Vetoing the card that claws ITSELF out — unearth,
escape, sarcophagus, "{B}: Return this card from your graveyard to the battlefield", a death trigger
that returns it — was measured and rejected on 09-19 (16 of 21 untagged, 7 points of recall) and
RULED IN on 2026-10-13: "mai Reanimate" for a card returning or casting itself (Helbrute, Wave of
Rats, Lunar Hatchling named; 22 more realigned and handed back: Balustrade Wurm, Bloodfeather
Phoenix, Necrosavant, Skeleton Crew …). Asked of Regrowth too the same day: a card returning itself
to HAND is not Regrowth either ("Mai Regrowth (come Reanimate)").

Read 2026-10-13, every wording tagged by hand on the reviewed cards that say it: a long list before
"from", "OF your graveyard" (Shallow Grave), a PUT of a described or PERMANENT card, the Aura that
brings back the card it enchants (Animate Dead), the verb first (Squirming Emergence),
hand-or-battlefield (Zuko's Conviction), the mass swap (Living Death), cards chosen in a graveyard
(Afterlife from the Loam), a library-and/or-graveyard search, every creature of yours that dies
(Valkyrie's Call). A creature put back under its OWNER's or an opponent's control is not this: it is
not yours (Immortal Obligation, Mysterious Limousine).

Ruled 2026-10-15 with Cheat ("Reanimate e Cheat non dovrebbero contare" for the tribal ones): a
creature only a TYPE can bring back is not this tag — a Pirate card (Admiral Brass, Unsinkable), a
Bird creature card (Kastral, the Windcrested), an Assassin whose token copies follow it (Altaïr
Ibn-La'Ahad) — nor is one behind a cost only OTHER members of the type pay (Gravespawn Sovereign's
five Zombies, Meanders Guide's other Merfolk); a "Saga card" reads the same way, as the hand had it
(Rydia, Summoner of Mist; OVERTURNED 2026-11-06, a Saga is no tribe). Not tribal: an exclusion
(Junji's non-Dragon), a card any creature could be in the same sentence (Thwart the Grave), a
Vehicle, Spacecraft or Cave card, one creature of the type
(Slimefoot and Squee's Saproling), a type the card makes tokens of (Rat King, Verminister). The 7
reviewed tribal Reanimates were handed back. A tribal trigger the card fires ITSELF stays Reanimate,
asked the same day ("Restano"): Vulpine Harvester is a Phyrexian and needs no tribal deck.

Read 2026-10-16: an enchanted creature that dies returned UNDER YOUR CONTROL (Necrogen Communion,
False Demise; Abduction returns it to its owner and is Steal), a creature put into your graveyard
returned to the battlefield (Angelic Renewal, moved from Regrowth), and a creature or permanent CAST
FREE from your graveyard (Counterpoint, and Shiko, Paragon of the Way's copy). Handed back: All
Hallow's Eve, Cass and False Demise with the tag; Noctis, Prince of Lucis, which casts artifacts
from your graveyard for their cost, without it.

Overturned 2026-10-30 with Buff and Cheat ("Se scegli tu il tipo quando gioco la carta è diverso da
un tribale fisso", for every tag): a creature card of the CHOSEN type, or "of that type" after the
choice, is any creature, and is Reanimate again. From the Rubble, untagged by hand, was realigned
and handed back; Haunting Voyage and Bloodline Bidding are proposed. A type printed on the card
stays tribal, and so does one SHARED with another creature.

Ruled 2026-10-30 with Regrowth (Anikthea, Hand of Erebos): a token copy of a CREATURE card exiled
from your graveyard stays Reanimate (Ardyn, Mordor on the March, Abyssal Harvester); a noncreature
card's copy is Regrowth (OVERTURNED 2026-11-06 for every permanent card: Anikthea is Reanimate).

Read 2026-11-05: a creature CARD exiled from a graveyard and put onto the battlefield when the
exiler leaves, "exile up to one other target creature from the battlefield or creature card from a
graveyard … put the exiled cards onto the battlefield under their owners' control" (Savior of
Ollenbock, Removal and Reanimate by hand, 1 of 1). It reads the graveyard half: the rule of
2026-10-13 that a card handed back to its OWNER is no reanimation (Mysterious Limousine, Vault 13)
is about a creature taken off the battlefield, while this card is named from a graveyard and comes
back from the dead.

Read 2026-11-06, the sitting of 11-03..05 and the older misses of the same wordings, each tagged by
hand: several creature cards chosen in a graveyard and returned "each of them" or "two of them at
random" in the next sentence (Sinister Waltz, The War in Heaven, 2 of 2); ALL the cards of a kind
returned, the verb first (Gerrard, Weatherlight Hero, 1 of 1; Faith's Reward, proposed the same,
loses it on 2026-11-12 because it is held up, below);
and a permanent card put from among the cards the card exiled from your graveyard (Winter, Cynical
Opportunist, 1 of 1). Not this: a token copy of THIS creature, "exile up to one target creature card
from an opponent's graveyard. If you do, create a token that's a copy of this creature" (Uchuulon),
or of a creature token you control, populate's reminder (Selesnya Eulogist): the token copies
nothing that was in the graveyard, so nobody comes back (both Tokens alone by hand, 0 of 2); and
cards exiled with a Vehicle returned "tapped under their owners' control" (Mechtitan Core), handed
back to their owners as Mysterious Limousine is.

RULED 2026-11-06, "No: dal grimorio non è Reanimate" (Journey to the Lost City), as a refinement of
the 09-19 route "a card THIS CARD exiled, put onto the battlefield": it does not hold when the card
exiled cards only from the top of a LIBRARY, since nothing came back from the dead. A card that
exiles from a graveyard as well keeps the tag (Yggdrasil, Rebirth Engine).

REDEFINED 2026-11-06 ("Sì, confermo"), from Reanimate the card: the paragraph at the top. Measured
on the reviewed cards before any code: an artifact, enchantment or Vehicle card back from the
graveyard was tagged on 30 of 35 (Brilliant Restoration, Greasefang, Argivian Restoration), a return
under a mana-value or power cap on 62 of 62, a return given back to the hand at the end step on 2 of
2 (Greasefang, Swift Warkite), while a cast from the graveyard that pays the cost was untagged on 15
of 21 (Regrowth, as Noctis; the 6 tagged also return a creature to the battlefield). Asked the same
day, one at a time, each answer below.

RULED 2026-11-06, "-Reanimate, +Steal": Sothera, the Supervoid's creature is exiled off an
opponent's battlefield, so nothing came back from the dead, and taking it is a theft (Steal.md,
Abstruse Appropriation). Profane Procession and Sisters of Stone Death read the same. With it the
09-19 route reads only an exile OF THE DEAD — a card exiled from a graveyard, or a creature exiled
as it dies or instead of dying (The Darkness Crystal, Gisa) — and that one test replaces the four
guards of 09-19, 10-13, 11-05 and 11-06: your own creature blinked (Cold Storage, Safe Haven; Synod
Sanctum and Northampton Farm lose the proposal), a permanent handed back to its owner (Mysterious
Limousine, Vault 13, Mechtitan Core), a library (Journey to the Lost City), and the graveyard half
of Savior of Ollenbock, which now simply passes.

RULED 2026-11-06, "+Reanimate a tutte e 3": an Aura or Equipment card put from your hand or
graveyard onto the battlefield, attached (Danitha, Benalia's Hope; Vault 101: Birthday Party;
Liberated Livestock), as Swift Warkite's creature card from hand or graveyard.

RULED 2026-11-06, "Saga non è tribale": the tribe is a creature type, the one a tribal deck is built
around, and a Saga is an enchantment as a Vehicle is an artifact. Rydia, Summoner of Mist is
Reanimate.

RULED 2026-11-06: a creature that BECOMES a copy of a card exiled from a graveyard is not this tag,
because nothing enters the battlefield (Mimeoplasm, Revered One, against Lazav, Wearer of Faces,
untagged). A token copy is, because a permanent enters.

RULED 2026-11-06, "Reanimate al posto di Regrowth": a token copy of a noncreature PERMANENT card
exiled from your graveyard (Anikthea, Hand of Erebos's enchantment, as Tamiyo, Compleated Sage's
nonland permanent card), overturning 10-30 for every permanent card.

Read 2026-11-06 under the definition, and handed back: Grasping Tentacles (an artifact card from an
opponent's graveyard), Nahiri, the Unforgiving (a token copy of a creature or Equipment card, exiled
at the end step), Leonardo da Vinci and Osgir, the Reconstructor (a token copy of an artifact card
from your graveyard). Read on the two misses, each Reanimate by hand: a real alternative cost,
Anrakyr the Traveller's life "rather than paying its mana cost" (as Cheat counts Leyline of
Mutation), and Crabomination's spell cast free from among the cards an opponent exiled, one of them
from their graveyard (OVERTURNED 2026-11-10, below). Not a card casting itself for an alternative
cost (Scourge of Nel Toth).

Read 2026-11-10, from the second sitting: Crabomination is Discard alone by hand. A spell cast free
from among the cards an opponent exiled from three zones at once (the top of their library, a card
at random from their graveyard and one from their hand) is not this tag, and the alternative written
for it on 11-06 is gone: no other card says it.

RULED 2026-11-08 with the Steal definition (Steal.md, Q2 "Solo Reanimate, anche i 4"): out of an
OPPONENT'S graveyard is this tag alone, never Steal as well (a card in a graveyard is no permanent),
the pair Necromancy and Coffin Queen against Ashen Powder and Geth's Summons; 17 cards lost Steal.
Two wordings read with it, each named: any permanent card CHOSEN in a graveyard and then put, "a
creature or planeswalker card in that player's graveyard. Put those cards" (Breach the Multiverse)
and "artifact or creature card in an opponent's graveyard that was put there from their library
this turn. Put it" (Captain N'ghathrod); and a creature card PUT INTO a graveyard and then put onto
the battlefield (Helm of Obedience, which mills until one turns up; Lorcan, Warlock Collector).
Colossal Grave-Reaver, the same with your own mill, reviewed untagged on 2026-09-10, was reached and
handed back; Grim Return and Scrounge are proposed the same.

RULED 2026-11-12, "Solo Protection": what died THIS TURN brought back from your graveyard by a card
you HOLD UP, an instant or an activation, is a save after the fact and Protection alone, not this
tag, though it returns to the battlefield. Brought Back, Othelm, Sigardian Outcast and Gerrard's
Hourglass Pendant were already Protection alone by hand. Revivify, Reanimate and Protection by hand
on 11-11, is reached and handed back; Faith's Reward, unreviewed, loses the tag. A TRIGGER is not
held up and stays this tag: "When Gerrard dies" (Gerrard, Weatherlight Hero) and "Whenever Moira
deals combat damage" (Moira, Urborg Haunt). Nor are the cards put from another player's graveyard
under your control (Thrilling Encore, Grim Return, Supper for Spiders), which keep it.

Read 2026-11-12: the targets chosen first and "put them onto the battlefield under your control" two
sentences later (Command the Dreadhorde, 1 of 1).
