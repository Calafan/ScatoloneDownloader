# Reanimate

Canonical record of the rulings for `CardEffect.Reanimate`. The one-line tooltip shown in the tagger
is in [`EffectGlossary.cs`](../../ScatoloneDownloader/Mtg/EffectGlossary.cs).

a creature back from the dead and straight onto the BATTLEFIELD, cheating its cost.

Three more routes ruled 2026-09-19, because the graveyard is not the only place one comes back from
and the tag was missing a third of what it should hold. A card THIS CARD EXILED, put onto the
battlefield under your control, is the same trick with a waiting room in the middle (Purgatory,
Sothera, The Darkness Crystal, Ghost Vacuum) — but blinking a creature YOU CONTROL is not, because
Cold Storage and Safe Haven exile your own and hand it back, which is a strange kind of protection
and stays untagged.

A TOKEN COPY of a creature card in a graveyard is not the card itself, but what lands on the table
is the same thing (Cursecloth Wrappings' embalm, Ardyn, Coiling Rebirth). And a LAND out of the
graveyard is not this tag (7 reviewed cards put one back and only 1 carried it) — nor Ramp, measured
2026-09-29 at 1 of 8 (Lumra the one): the human files it under Regrowth (Undergrowth Recon) or
nothing. Vetoing the card that claws ITSELF out — unearth, escape, sarcophagus, "{B}: Return this
card from your graveyard to the battlefield", a death trigger that returns it — was measured and
rejected on 09-19 (16 of 21 untagged, 7 points of recall) and RULED IN on 2026-10-13: "mai
Reanimate" for a card returning or casting itself (Helbrute, Wave of Rats, Lunar Hatchling named; 22
more realigned and handed back: Balustrade Wurm, Bloodfeather Phoenix, Necrosavant, Skeleton Crew
…). Asked of Regrowth too the same day: a card returning itself to HAND is not Regrowth either ("Mai
Regrowth (come Reanimate)").

Read 2026-10-13, every wording tagged by hand on the reviewed cards that say it: a long list before
"from", "OF your graveyard" (Shallow Grave), a PUT of a described or PERMANENT card, the Aura that
brings back the card it enchants (Animate Dead), the verb first (Squirming Emergence),
hand-or-battlefield (Zuko's Conviction), the mass swap (Living Death), cards chosen in a graveyard
(Afterlife from the Loam), a library-and/or-graveyard search, every creature of yours that dies
(Valkyrie's Call). A creature put back under its OWNER's or an opponent's control is not this: it is
not yours (Immortal Obligation, Mysterious Limousine).

Ruled 2026-10-15 with Cheat ("Reanimate e Cheat non dovrebbero contare" for the tribal ones): a
creature only a TYPE can bring back is not this tag — a Pirate card (Admiral Brass, Unsinkable), a
Bird creature card (Kastral, the Windcrested), a creature card of the chosen type (From the Rubble)
or of that type (Haunting Voyage), an Assassin whose token copies follow it (Altaïr Ibn-La'Ahad) —
nor is one behind a cost only OTHER members of the type pay (Gravespawn Sovereign's five Zombies,
Meanders Guide's other Merfolk); a "Saga card" reads the same way, as the hand had it (Rydia,
Summoner of Mist). Not tribal: an exclusion (Junji's non-Dragon), a card any creature could be in
the same sentence (Thwart the Grave), a Vehicle, Spacecraft or Cave card, one creature of the type
(Slimefoot and Squee's Saproling), a type the card makes tokens of (Rat King, Verminister). The 7
reviewed tribal Reanimates were handed back. A tribal trigger the card fires ITSELF stays Reanimate,
asked the same day ("Restano"): Vulpine Harvester is a Phyrexian and needs no tribal deck.

Read 2026-10-16: an enchanted creature that dies returned UNDER YOUR CONTROL (Necrogen Communion,
False Demise; Abduction returns it to its owner and is Steal), a creature put into your graveyard
returned to the battlefield (Angelic Renewal, moved from Regrowth), and a creature or permanent CAST
FREE from your graveyard (Counterpoint, and Shiko, Paragon of the Way's copy). Handed back: All
Hallow's Eve, Cass and False Demise with the tag; Noctis, Prince of Lucis, which casts artifacts
from your graveyard for their cost, without it.
