# Buff

Canonical record of the rulings for `CardEffect.Buff`. The one-line tooltip shown in the tagger is
in [`EffectGlossary.cs`](../../ScatoloneDownloader/Mtg/EffectGlossary.cs).

DEFINITION, 2026-11-13 ("Sì, così", Glorious Anthem): ANOTHER creature of yours, or the team, gets
bigger — power and toughness, +1/+1 counters, double strike, a Role, a new base — when making it
bigger is what the card is FOR: the pump stays (an anthem, an Aura, an Equipment), it repeats, it is
big (more than +1/+1), or it is the whole card. Out: the card itself, a tribe or tokens alone, a
keyword other than double strike, a bite (Removal), a small pump said beside a shield (Protection).
The rulings below are readings of it. Three exceptions are kept with their reason: on a CREATURE the
small repeated pump that wears off is an extra of a body judged as a whole (B3, 45 of 45 reviewed on
2026-10-14); COLORLESS is a tribe, a payoff only a colorless deck collects; and a TEAM BASE sets
rather than adds (2026-11-13, at the end).

raise POWER and/or TOUGHNESS — that is the whole test, ruled 2026-09-18. A granted KEYWORD is not
Buff, however good it is: flying, deathtouch, lifelink, first strike, indestructible change what a
creature does, not what its numbers are, and the hand-tagging says so 49 cards to 5 (Jump and Cloak
of Feathers are the same sentence and only one was tagged).

DOUBLE STRIKE is the single exception, because doubling the damage is doubling the power by another
name. A pump sized by X or by a count is a pump too. Only when it lands on SOMETHING ELSE (Giant
Growth, Glorious Anthem, Bonesplitter): a creature that pumps only itself has a stat line, not an
effect, so "{2}{R}: This creature gets +3/+0" and "Uril gets +2/+2 for each Aura attached to it"
stay untagged. A +1/+1 COUNTER on somebody else is the same effect in the other vocabulary and
counts too, ruled 2026-09-15 — one counter on one creature, support, distribute, all of it; only
counters aimed at an OPPONENT'S creatures stay out. That ruling went AGAINST the measurement: of the
161 reviewed cards putting a counter on somebody else only 46 were tagged, the two halves were
indistinguishable by wording, and 113 reviewed entries were realigned by hand the same day. It is
the largest single correction the ontology has taken. The other half of that ruling points the other
way: a pump restricted to one creature TYPE is NOT Buff — "Minotaur creatures get +1/+0" promises a
payoff a cube with no Minotaur deck cannot collect.

A COLOUR is not a tribe (Crusade, Bad Moon keep it) and neither is a STATE (Castle's "untapped
creatures you control"); a lord that also pumps something unrestricted keeps it too.

SIZE AND PURPOSE, ruled 2026-09-24/25, and it OVERTURNS "one counter on one creature" from
2026-09-15 for one-shots. The human's rule: Buff only when repeated, permanent (not tribal), at
instant speed, over an area, or +5/+5 and more — and "a single one must be bigger than +1/+1 or have
more than one target", where SINGLE means ONE-SHOT, not one target: the human's own re-review of
Buff that day kept Expanding Ooze and Sample Collector (one counter, one target, every attack) and
Fae Flight (+1/+0, permanent). Three rulings then fixed what SMALL (+1/+1, +1/+0, one counter) is
worth, each asking what the card is FOR. B1: a small one-shot at sorcery speed is never Buff — "1
segnalino o un +1/+1 a sorcery non fa niente", and most of them are creatures entering. B2: a small
one-shot at INSTANT speed is Buff only when the pump IS the card — Gift of the Viper and Guided
Strike, not Magic Damper or Lightfoot Technique, which are Protection with a rider; read as "carries
no other effect tag", with Filter not counting, since a scry is the rider the human calls saturating
(Storm Strike keeps it). B3: a small REPEATED pump on a CREATURE is not Buff either — "an extra of a
creature you judge as a whole"; on a NONCREATURE the pump is the card and stays (Firebreathing,
Innkeeper's Talent, Collector's Cage). And a BITE is Removal alone at any size (Felling Blow, Bite
Down on Crime): the pump is how the removal is aimed. Stonewood Invocation, +5/+5 and shroud, is
Buff and Protection. 105 reviewed tags came off (64 B3, 27 B1, 8 B2, 5 bites, and one self-pump
found on the way) and were handed back to the human unreviewed, at their request, because the rule
had only settled in the last 200–250 cards of the sitting that produced it. Only the pumps the size
reading recognises are judged; a double strike grant or a base P/T is left standing and
keeps the tag on its own (Origin of Spider-Man's chapter III). A bullet that carries its own trigger
is timed by it (Hollowmurk Siege's "Abzan — Whenever you attack"), and a cost that exiles the card
from its graveyard runs once (Wither and Bloom). B3 was CONFIRMED for noncreatures the same day: the
30 reviewed noncreature cards whose only pump is small and repeated "fanno solo Buff" and keep it.

Ruled by name as well: a STATE in front of a tribe is still a tribe (Gornog's "Attacking Warriors
you control"), and counters that ARE a body — on the creatures the sentence just made (Valgavoth's
Onslaught) or on a noncreature that becomes a 0/0 (Case of the Filched Falcon) — are Tokens, not
Buff; "those creatures" that were TARGETED rather than made stay Buff (Biogenic Upgrade, Smile at
Death). Hurska Sweet-Tooth, Soulshriek and Endurance Bobblehead are Buff, the last with Protection.

The self-pump ruling of 2026-09-18 was also found missing three wordings and they are read now: "it
gets" when the clause in front of it is about the card (Slimy Piper, "as long as this creature is
equipped, it gets"), the card's own name ("Rashka gets"), and counters doubled on this creature
(Mossborn Hydra).

On the human's re-review of 2024 (2026-09-25) the TRIBE was ruled out in every vocabulary, not only
"Goblins you control get": counters on each of a tribe (Camellia's Squirrels, Kastral's Birds), a
counter the tribe enters with (Slinza's Beasts), and a target that must belong to it (Inside
Source's "target Detective", Rockface Village's list of four types) — and TOKENS are a tribe by
another name (Hildibrand's "creature tokens you control get +1/+1", Sandstorm Salvager's counter on
each creature token), so a pump only tokens get is a Tokens payoff and not Buff. The 12 older
reviewed tags on the wrong side (Krenko, Minwu, Caretaker's Talent, Ezekiel Sims…) were taken off
and handed back. The same re-review put the tag back on the one repeated small pump on a creature
that grows ANOTHER each time it connects (Prowler, Scurry of Squirrels — 2 of 2, against 61 B3
creatures confirmed without it), and found the reading blind to double strike beside a quoted token
pump (Blacksmith's Talent), to the creature just CAST entering with counters (Communal Brewing — but
not the one a reanimation brings back), to scavenge granted to a graveyard (Young Deathclaws), to a
counter "on a creature you control" in a mode bought five times (Season of Gathering), and to a new
base on a stolen creature, which is the theft's contour (Coerced to Kill). Four more rulings closed
that pass the same day.

A SPELL that is only the pump is Buff however small, sorcery speed included — "se la carta fa solo
quello … che diventi Buff anche se fa schifo" (Honor); B1 now means a sorcery that does something
else too.

COLORLESS counts as a tribe (Kozilek; It That Heralds the End's older tag corrected by name, Titans'
Vanguard and Vannifar handed back); other colours still do not.

COUNTERS a creature hands out as it ENTERS need three or more — Apothecary Stomper's two are "come
se fosse un 6/6" — while a temporary pump on entry (Toucan-Puffin) and counters from the graveyard
(Agent of Kotis) keep the old bar. And a small pump that lasts the turn and is made only at sorcery
speed is "irrilevante come effetto" on a card that does something else: Starnheim Memento is Ramp. A
sorcery-speed COUNTER is not that (Perilous Snare keeps Buff). A pump for the creatures that share
the card's NAME is the card pumping its own copies, not this tag (Gruff Triplets, untagged by the
human on 2026-09-29; Charmed Stray).

Read again 2026-10-14, four rulings. B3 NARROWED: a creature that hands ANOTHER creature a +1/+1
counter or a Role every turn is Buff ("tutti Buff": Galadriel, Gift-Giver, Gylwain, Haliya, Haazda
Vigilante); the small repeated pump that wears off at end of turn stays out — measured, dropping B3
whole moved 45 more reviewed cards and every one against the hand tag. Mentor is that counter (Iroh,
Ordruun Mentor; Danny Pink and Tributary Instructor handed back).

A ROLE is a pump with no numbers (Become Brutes, Ellivere, Spellbook Vendor); a printed Role token
card carries no tags, like ante.

MOVING a +1/+1 counter off an artifact or enchantment onto a creature is Buff (Explorer's Cache,
Agent's Toolkit; Costume Closet, Afiya Grove handed back). A quoted pump handed to the equipped or
enchanted creature or to the team is the card's (Idolized, Well Rested, Conformer Shuriken,
Astrologian's Planisphere, Tale of Katara and Toph; Red Mage's Rapier handed back, 4 to 1). Granting
PROWESS is a keyword, not Buff (Narset, Enlightened Exile handed back without it), and a team base
X/X is not Buff either (Candlekeep Inspiration handed back). A base that comes with defender or the
loss of every ability is Pacify (Stasis Field). More tribes: "Ninja and Rogue creatures", "each Rat
you control gets", "target Halfling or Treefolk", "each Warrior creature", a tribe's spell entering
with a counter (Torgal), "if it's a Spider, put" (Aunt May); Coppercoat Vanguard, Oakhollow Village
and Vaan handed back without Buff. A creature's one-shot "for each" is judged by its printed number
(Eastfarthing Farmer). Measured: Buff F1 96.6 to 98.6, with the 66 reviewed cards these rulings
reached handed back to the tagger.

RULED 2026-10-16 ("Solo Protection", Presumed Dead against Fake Your Own Death): a one-shot pump
said in the same breath as a shield — indestructible, hexproof, shroud, protection, or "When this
creature dies, return it" — is the shield's rider on a card that is Protection, not Buff. Dark
Endurance and Fake Your Own Death handed back without it; Hajar's "Sacrifice Hajar" is the same
one-shot. A +5/+5 is a pump of its own (Stonewood Invocation), a repeated one is an engine
(Endurance Bobblehead), a creature's entry trigger that is not Protection keeps the tag (Rampaging
Spiketail, Perigee Beckoner), and so does a pump alone on its line beside a shield only a Human gets
(Flare of Faith).

RULED 2026-10-16 ("Keyword, mai Buff"): exalted and rampage are keywords, never Buff, like prowess
(Emissary of Soulfire, Merchant of Truth and Rapid Fire handed back without it).

RULED 2026-11-05 ("Keyword, mai Buff"): MELEE is a keyword like exalted, rampage and prowess,
never Buff, whatever its reminder says it does. Vivien's Stampede ("Each creature you control
gains vigilance, trample, and melee until end of turn", tagged Buff on 11-03) is against Skyhunter
Strike Force (the Lieutenant hands other creatures melee), Drogskol Reinforcements (Spirits) and
Depthshaker Titan (artifact creatures; Tokens alone), all untagged. Vivien's Stampede is named and
its Buff is to be taken off by hand; the draw it carries is CardAdvantage (see that file).

RULED 2026-10-16 ("Solo sulle tue creature"): a +N/-M is Buff only on creatures you control —
Ashnod's Battle Gear's "target creature you control gets +2/-2", Tourach's Gate's attackers. An
Aura's or an Equipment's +N/-M is not (Grave Servitude handed back).

Read 2026-10-16, wordings the rules could not see: double strike handed to the creature named in the
sentence before (Kayla's Command), as a mode of the equipped creature's attack (The Spear of
Leonidas), or in the team's list of keywords (Bleeding Effect); a counter on the creature a trigger
names (Sphere Grid, Experimental Lab) or that the sentence before targeted (The Aetherspark), but
not on the one just handed to an opponent (Jon Irenicus); keyword counters joined by "and" (Guide of
Souls); a permanent pump beside a damage shield (Caduceus); reinforce (Fowl Strike); the card
sacrificed by name (Hajar); "Otherwise" is not "other" (Lily Bowen); and a tribe written as a
clause, "that's a Skeleton or Pirate" (Skeleton Crew).

MEASURED 2026-10-16: a ONE-SHOT counter that comes with a keyword counter is the keyword handed out.
The three reviewed cards whose only pump it is are untagged (Sagu Pummeler, Kheru Goldkeeper,
Champion of Dusan), against Agent of Kotis's renew without the reach counter, tagged and reviewed
the same day as Sagu Pummeler. Repeated, it is an engine (Guide of Souls).

REJECTED 2026-10-16, then RULED the other way the same day: the quoted target pump handed to an
enchanted LAND as the card's own pump. Mystic Might was tagged on 09-04, before the 2026-09-21
ruling whose own case is Forbidden Lore, the same Aura; Mystic Might was handed back, confirmed with
Buff, and asked ("Buff a entrambe", U1): a land is no creature to own the pump, so the Aura hands it
out.
This overturns the 09-21 case for an enchanted land only, and Forbidden Lore is handed back with the
tag; a pump granted to a creature or a token still belongs to it.

Handed back 2026-10-16 to agree with older rulings that reach them: without Buff a keyword grant
(Flickering Ward, The Wind Crystal), a self-pump (Nesting Bot's "Max speed — This creature gets"), a
tribe's target (Manifold Mouse), a team base (Sephiroth, Fallen Hero) and a token's pump (Crescent
Island Temple, Unfortunate Accident, We Ride at Dawn); with it, B3 narrowed (Shelinda, Yevon
Acolyte; Spider-Man Noir). Measured: Buff F1 98.6 to 99.1 before the hand-backs, and the 17 reviewed
cards still in disagreement are exactly the ones handed back.

RULED 2026-10-27 ("Buff", Tarrian's Soulcleaver against Greatsword of Tyr and Lost Jitte): a +1/+1
counter put on the EQUIPPED creature again and again is Buff whatever sets it off, anybody's death
included. Tarrian's, untagged by hand, is corrected by name; Blade of the Bloodchief, Ghost Lantern
and the five upkeep Rings (Ring of Thune...) follow unreviewed.

RULED 2026-10-27 ("Svista, niente Buff"): The Meep's "creatures you control have base power and
toughness X/X" is the team base of 2026-10-14 and stays out, corrected by name. And Halsin, Emerald
Archdruid's token made a 4/4 Bear is Tokens, not Buff (see Tokens.md).

Read 2026-10-27, on the sitting of 10-20..26: a counter on ANY attacking creature the card pays for
(Anointer of Valor), on the creature CHOSEN the sentence before (Dig Deep, Elspeth Resplendent's
+1), and on the equipped creature after its own trigger (Greatsword of Tyr) or in a mode (Lost
Jitte). A new BASE for one creature you control is Buff when it is the whole card (Scale Up's 6/4
Wurm), B2 asked the other way round; as one mode of a Protection spell it is not (Secret Identity).
A pump beside a return from death OR EXILE is the shield's rider (Ashnod's Intervention). More
tribes: a hyphen in the type (Autonomous Assembler's Assembly-Worker), the tribe as a condition ("if
that creature is a Goat, it also gets", Goatnap), as a clause after "each creature you control"
(Kibo's Apes and Monkeys) and as the plural target of distributed counters (Numa's Elves). The tribe
a card makes tokens of ITSELF is still a tribe for this tag: the reviewed cards that pump their own
tokens' type are untagged (Gisa, Arahbo, Krenko, Baron of Tin Street, Corpses of the Lost, Baru),
and Vault 12: The Necropolis, tagged on 09-12 before the tribe was ruled out, is handed back without
it. Keyword counters listed without "and" (Scavenged Brawler's four +1/+1, flying, vigilance,
trample and lifelink) are the keyword handed out on a creature, as measured on 2026-10-16, but a
SPELL that is only such counters is B2's own example and keeps the tag (Gift of the Viper;
Unexpected Fangs follows).

Overturned 2026-10-30 ("Se scegli tu il tipo quando gioco la carta è diverso da un tribale fisso",
for every tag): a creature type CHOSEN as the card enters or is cast is no tribe. Its anthem is Buff
like any other, as Lifecraft Engine's already was by hand. Patchwork Banner, Banner of Kinship,
Etchings of the Chosen, Icon of Ancestry, Morophon, the Boundless and Renewed Solidarity, untagged
by hand, were realigned and handed back. A type PRINTED on the card stays a tribe (Karumonix's
Rats), and so does one SHARED with another creature.

Read 2026-10-30: counters COPIED from this card onto the creature that enters (Denry Klin, Editor in
Chief), and a pump with LIFELINK beside a granted shield, which keeps Buff beside Protection where a
bare pump is the shield's rider (Return to Action, as Scales of Shale; Presumed Dead stays
Protection alone). Not this: the counters a TRIGGER waits for ("whenever you put one or more +1/+1
counters on another colorless creature", Omarthis, Ghostfire Initiate; 0 of 4 reviewed), and a
counter on "target Shrine for each Shrine you control" (Go-Shintai of Boundless Vigor), a tribe like
Treebeard's (0 of 11).

RULED 2026-11-05 (asked on Simian Simulacrum and Kinsbaile Courier; the same ruling as Tokens of
2026-10-27 and CardAdvantage): UNEARTH and ENCORE SUM the entries of a card for Buff. The card
enters once, once more with an unearth and twice more with an encore (the cube is played 2vs2, so
encore makes a copy for each of two opponents, "encore vale doppio"), and the counters its enters
trigger puts on a creature are summed over those entries before they meet the bar of three that
counters on entry have to reach. Simian Simulacrum's two counters are four with its unearth: Buff
beside Burn by hand. Kinsbaile Courier's one counter is three with its encore: reached, and its
hand tag (Burn alone, 10-20) is to be handed back Buff. COUNTERS only. The hypothesis that the
entries multiply EVERY pump an enters trigger makes, the temporary ones too, was measured and
moved no card (11356 exact either way), so it is not written. The keywords are asked of the text
without its reminder, which names both again.

RULED 2026-11-05 ("Buff se forza >= 3"): a new BASE power and toughness for ANY target creature,
not only one you control, is Buff when it is the whole card (a cantrip "Draw a card" is no other
effect) and the base POWER is three or more. It extends the Scale Up reading of 2026-10-27 (one
creature of yours, 6/4), which still stands. Serpentine Ambush (5/5, tagged by hand) is named; Suit
Up (4/5 and a card, untagged 2026-10-23) is reached and its hand tag is to be handed back Buff;
Startling Development (4/4 and cycling), Mordenkainen's Polymorph (4/4 and flying) and Omnibian
({T}: 3/3, repeated) are unreviewed and follow. A SHRINK stays out, a base power under three being
the creature taken down: Metamorphic Blast's 0/1 mode (CardAdvantage and Pacify) and Eccentric
Apprentice's 1/1 Bird. Omnibian is a repeated one-turn effect on a creature, which B3 would keep out
for a pump; it follows the ruling as written and is flagged to the human.

Read 2026-11-05, on the sitting of 11-03..05, each under a ruling above. DOUBLING the power of a
tribe ("double the power of each Dragon you control", Thrakkus the Butcher) is a pump restricted to
a type, untagged by hand, where Double Trouble's "each creature you control" stays tagged. A
self-pump whose AS LONG AS condition says "another" is still the card's own (Hungry Ridgewolf,
untagged: "As long as you control another Wolf or Werewolf, this creature gets +1/+0" had "another"
read as the creature pumped); seven unreviewed cards (Bant Sureblade, Bristlebane Outrider, Esper
Stormblade, Grixis Grimblade, Jund Hackblade, Naya Hushblade, Thrash of Raptors) lose the proposal,
and no reviewed card of that shape is tagged.

The quoted pump handed through one step (2026-10-14) reaches a BACKGROUND's static "Commander
creatures you own have ... \"This creature gets +X/+0\"" (Criminal Past, tagged beside Hardy
Outlander and Master Chef) and an EMBLEM that pumps "creatures you control" (Ellywick
Tumblestrum's -7; Ajani Resolute, Elspeth, Sun's Champion, Garruk, Cursed Huntsman, Gideon, Ally
of Zendikar, Oko, Lorwyn Liege, Sorin, Lord of Innistrad and Vivien Reid unreviewed follow).
Measured and narrowed: the Background wording widened to ANY quoted counter or pump made four
reviewed cards Buff against the hand (Agent of the Shadow Thieves and Clan Crafter put a counter
on themselves, Haunted One and Tavern Brawler pump for a turn behind a trigger), so only the
static pump is read. A TRIBE's emblem is the tribe's pump (Kaito, Saheeli), and Baldur's Gate
Wilderness's is a Dungeon's.

The NAME of a mode is no fight: You're Ambushed on the Road's "Stand and Fight" bullet is a +1/+3
pump (Buff and Protection by hand) that "fights" had read as a bite and as an answer on its line;
Summon: Magus Sisters keeps its Buff and School Daze has no pump. Slip Out the Back's counter is the
shield's rider (Protection alone, see Protection.md).

Read 2026-11-12 under "Buff se forza >= 3" (2026-11-05): a big base the target creature HAS rather
than becomes, Square Up's instant "Target creature has base power and toughness 4/4 until end of
turn" (Buff by hand). It reaches four cards reviewed before that ruling and untagged, handed back:
Dreadful as the Storm (5/5), Zhalfirin Shapecraft (4/3), Genemorph Imago (3/3 or 6/6 on landfall)
and Gnottvold Hermit (5/5 on its attack). Read the same day: an activation that names its target and
puts the counter "on it" (Tyrite Sanctum, the repeated counter). Out: the counters that size what
the card ANIMATED (Rise and Shine's 0/0 artifacts with four counters, Tokens alone; 0 of 4 reviewed
with Animating Faerie, Emergent Sequence and Workshop Elders), and a tribe's anthem behind an
ability word's dash (Scuttletide's "Delirium — Crabs you control get +1/+1"; History of Benalia's
Knights and Nut Collector's Squirrels among the unreviewed).

RULED 2026-11-13 ("Basta 2 per entrambi", Apothecary Stomper against Toucan-Puffin): COUNTERS a
creature hands out as it enters meet the bar of TWO, like a temporary pump on entry (10 of 10
tagged). This OVERTURNS the bar of three of 2026-09-25 ("come se fosse un 6/6"), under which a
permanent +2/+2 counted for less than a temporary +2/+0. Apothecary Stomper and Caldaia Strongarm
are named; bolster 2 (Sandsteppe Scavenger, Elite Scaleguard), Roalesk, Apex Hybrid and Dimension X
Pizzasaur, unreviewed, follow. Unearth and encore still sum the entries (2026-11-05).

RULED 2026-11-13 ("Fuori, col perché"): a TEAM BASE, "creatures you control have base power and
toughness X/X", stays out because it SETS rather than adds: it raises the small and lowers the big,
and the team is not made bigger. 0 of 9 reviewed are tagged (Candlekeep Inspiration, The Meep,
Katara, Water Tribe's Hope, Graaz, The Capitoline Triad...). The base for ONE creature stays Buff as
ruled on 2026-10-27 and 2026-11-05.

RULED 2026-11-13 ("Equipaggiamento Buff"): an EQUIPMENT's +N/-M is Buff, because an Equipment goes
only on your creatures, which is the reason of "Solo sulle tue creature" (2026-10-16). Barbed
Batterfist is named; Barbed Battlegear follows. An Aura's +N/-M stays out: it can kill an X/1 of
theirs (Grave Servitude, Removal). Skullclamp, reached and asked, is Buff beside its CardAdvantage
("Buff e CardAdvantage"), though its -1 is how it kills your X/1 for the cards.

RULED 2026-11-13 ("Warp somma le entrate", Drix Fatemaker against Rayblade Trooper): WARP sums the
entries for Buff like unearth, as Tokens and CardAdvantage already read it as a second cast. Both
cards put one counter as they enter, two with the warp: Drix keeps its tag of 2026-09-10 and Rayblade
Trooper, untagged on 09-25, is named. Weftblade Enhancer was Buff already for its two targets.

Read 2026-11-13: a counter WITH which a creature is named is a condition, not a pump over an area
(Duskshell Crawler's "Each creature you control with a +1/+1 counter on it has trample", untagged
2026-11-11; Pridemalkin and Vigorbloom Vanguard, unreviewed, lose the proposal). And the bigger
count only a tribe gets INSTEAD is the tribe's, the one counter for everybody being what is judged
(Wakandan Royal Guard's "If that creature is another Hero, put two +1/+1 counters on it instead";
Elder Cathar's Human, unreviewed). Not asked: a PREPARED creature's spell face that is only a small
pump (Vigorbloom's Seed Suture, a counter and 1 life), no reviewed prepared card having one.
