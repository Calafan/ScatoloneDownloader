# Sacrifice

Canonical record of the rulings for `CardEffect.Sacrifice`. The one-line tooltip shown in the tagger
is in [`EffectGlossary.cs`](../../ScatoloneDownloader/Mtg/EffectGlossary.cs).

a sacrifice OUTLET for your own creatures (Ashnod's Altar, Goblin Bombardment, Viscera Seer) — the
half of the combo that makes a stolen creature worth taking, so read it next to Steal.

NOT an edict, which empties somebody else's board (that is Removal's business), and not "as an
additional cost to cast this spell, sacrifice a creature", which is a price paid once rather than a
place to put things. Creatures, artifacts and anything named as a permanent all count — Atog and
Infernal Tribute are Ashnod's Altar with different fuel — but LANDS do not: "sacrifice a land" is
Harrow paying for a fetch, never an engine.

Ruled 2026-09-15.

Narrowed to REPEATABLE on 2026-09-25 — "sì, togliamo Sacrifice alle carte una tantum": the human had
tagged one-shots 36 times up to 2026-09-23 and none of the 50 they met on the 25th, so an entering
"you may sacrifice another creature" (Boilerbilges Ripper), a spell's "sacrifice a creature. If you
do" (Tip the Scales), kicker, "rather than pay", exploit, casualty, devour and bargain are all out,
and so is "any number" made once (Angelic Aberration). What repeats is in: a cost in front of a
colon, a trigger that sacrifices every time (Kylox's attacks), a sacrifice forced EVERY UPKEEP
("anche se c'è un sacrificio ad ogni mantenimento": Lord of the Pit, Yawgmoth Demon, and Marjhan's
upkeep-only cost), a card CAST AGAIN for it ("se si rilancia sì": Worthy Cause's buyback, Wickerfolk
Indomitable), a loyalty ability, an equip cost, and a sacrificing keyword HANDED ON (Colonel
Autumn's exploit). The fuel is "creature o artefatti": Orcish Mechanics and The Gold Saucer count,
Zuran Orb's land was corrected by name, and an artifact TOKEN (Sophia cashing her own Clues) stays a
resource. Destroying or exiling your OWN permanent at will is the same outlet by another verb
(Despotic Scepter, Rats of Rath, City of Shadows). 40 reviewed tags moved (28 one-shots off, 12
repeatables on) and were handed back; Zuran Orb stayed reviewed. The re-review of the 40 kept 39 and
turned one, the same evening: a trigger the OPPONENT fires is a price, not an outlet — "Oath of
Lim-Dûl è un effetto extra non è a comando" — so "whenever you lose life, sacrifice" is out, and
Lich's Tomb with it; damage is the same price, named the same evening ("anche il Phyrexian Negator
rientra nella categoria"), so Phyrexian Negator, Phyrexian Totem and Lich's "whenever … is dealt
damage, sacrifice that many" are out too. An upkeep still counts: it comes every turn whatever the
opponent does.
