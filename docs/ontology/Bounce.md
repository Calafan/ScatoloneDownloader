# Bounce

Canonical record of the rulings for `CardEffect.Bounce`. The one-line tooltip shown in the tagger is
in [`EffectGlossary.cs`](../../ScatoloneDownloader/Mtg/EffectGlossary.cs).

**Definition, 2026-11-08: a NONLAND permanent that can be somebody else's (a target, all or each,
or the one an opponent chooses) leaves the battlefield but stays its OWNER'S, who has to play it
again: in hand, on or near the top of their library (their choice of top or bottom included), or
in an exile they may play it from (airbend, Extraordinary Journey). Tempo, not removal. Unsummon is
the card.** Out: the card itself as a cost or a drawback; your own permanents alone (Protection);
lands (LandDestruction, or Wipe with the board); spells (Counter); CARDS in a graveyard or in exile
(Bag of Holding); the bottom of the library or a shuffle, which is for good (RemovePermanent,
Removal); phasing; a blink. Accepted as proposed ("Sì, così").

Every ruling below is coherent with the definition; the answers of that day are at the end.
Disempower and Leadership Vacuum are the two cards corrected by name.

Before 2026-11-08: unsummon / boomerang — a TARGETED return to hand (or a mass one). "Return this creature to its
owner's hand" as an activation cost or an end-step drawback is a price the card pays, not an answer
it offers.

Narrowed 2026-09-15. Returning every LAND (or every Island) sets a mana base back and is not this
tag: Omen of Fire is Wipe and LandDestruction, ruled 2026-09-28.

Read 2026-09-29, each shape tagged on every reviewed card using it: a target put ON TOP of its
owner's library (Time Ebb, Ether Well), SECOND or THIRD FROM THE TOP (Deem Inferior, Lost Days,
Riptide Gearhulk — a target, not the card itself as the God-Eternals do, nor a card from a hand as
Lost Hours does), "the OWNER OF TARGET … puts it on their choice of the top or bottom" (Riverwalk
Technique, Sudden Setback), the owner as the target (Hurkyl's Recall), and creatures the opponents
chose (Summon: Valefor). Not this, the same day: a permanent YOU CONTROL returned to hand (3 of 31
reviewed cards tagged — Clement, Hallowed Ground, Snow Hound, taken to the human; saving one in
response is Protection's), a single LAND (0 of 5 — Active Volcano, Flash Flood), the Auras on your
own permanent (Scarab of the Unseen), and a card exiled WITH the card coming back (Nyla). A clause
that names one of theirs beside yours still bounces theirs (Aether Tradewinds).

Ruled the same day: AIRBEND where an opponent's permanent can be is this tag (Airbending Lesson,
Aang, the Last Airbender, Avatar's Wrath), airbending your OWN is Protection (Airbender's Reversal,
Appa, Steadfast Guardian); the three reviewed cards returning your own permanent were slips
(Clement, Hallowed Ground, Snow Hound); an Aura's creature returned is this tag however it is paid
(Phantom Wings, Sun Clasp); a permanent exiled until the card leaves or dies and then returned to
its owner's hand is this tag, and Regrowth for the graveyard cards exiled with it (Aurelia's
Vindicator, The Spot); Fleeting Effigy's end-step return is Burn like the Viashinos, and Krile
Baldesion's tag was a misclick for Regrowth.

Read 2026-10-27: the other word order of Extraordinary Journey, "For as long as that card remains
exiled, its owner may play it", on "up to one other target tapped creature or Vehicle" (Prowl,
Stoic Strategist).

Ruled 2026-11-08 with the RemovePermanent definition: a nonland PERMANENT exiled while its owner
may play or CAST it is this tag, a bounce with a tax like airbend (Soul Partition, RemovePermanent
by hand, corrected by name; Aerial Extortionist, Bounce by hand). See RemovePermanent.md, Q2.

Read 2026-11-08 under the definition, each a wording the rules missed: ANY nonland permanent put on
top of its owner's library, not a creature alone (Disempower, "target artifact or enchantment",
untagged by hand on 09-08 and corrected by name; Set Adrift, Vanishment, Banishment Decree,
Metamorphose, Temporal Eddy's "creature or land", Dovin's Dismissal's "up to one"); the owner of a
longer target list (Endless Detour, Bounce by hand) or of "up to one other target" (Plan for All
Outcomes); "and/or" and "non-Spirit" in the list (Sea God's Scorn, Baral's Expertise, Roaming
Ghostlight; Specimen Freighter's "non-Spacecraft creatures", untagged by hand on 09-10, handed
back); an Aura's creature put third from the top (Shattered Ego, as Phantom Wings). And two that are
not a permanent: every card exiled WITH the card ("return all cards exiled with" — Bag of Holding,
untagged by hand; Asmodeus, Northampton Farm) and a CARD from a graveyard, which is Regrowth
(Disturbing Plot, Pulse of Murasa, Saprazzan Bailiff; 10 proposed, none reviewed). "All creatures
on the battlefield and all creature cards in graveyards" still returns the board (Soulquake).

Q1, 2026-11-08: a commander returned to the command zone is NOT this tag. A cube game has no
commander, as ruled for Lieutenant on 2026-10-15 ("mai conta, per nessun tag"): Leadership Vacuum,
Bounce by hand, corrected by name to no tag.

Q2, 2026-11-08: an exile its owner may play only UNTIL THE END OF THEIR NEXT TURN is this tag,
played again at once like Soul Partition (Suspend Aggression, proposed RemovePermanent before).

Q3, 2026-11-08, for every tag: a CLEAVE card is read with the words in square brackets and without
them, and keeps what either reading earns ("cleave pagato, ogni tag"). Alchemist's Retrieval's
"Return target nonland permanent [you control]" is Protection printed and Bounce cleaved; Lunar
Rejection's "[Wolf or Werewolf] creature" is an Unsummon. Wash Away, the one reviewed cleave card
with a tag, was already Counter by hand for its cleaved reading. It moves other tags too: Fierce
Retribution and Parasitic Grasp +Removal, Dig Up +Tutor (and ManaFixing printed).
