# Bounce

Canonical record of the rulings for `CardEffect.Bounce`. The one-line tooltip shown in the tagger is
in [`EffectGlossary.cs`](../../ScatoloneDownloader/Mtg/EffectGlossary.cs).

unsummon / boomerang — a TARGETED return to hand (or a mass one). "Return this creature to its
owner's hand" as an activation cost or an end-step drawback is a price the card pays, not an answer
it offers.

Narrowed 2026-09-15. Returning every LAND (or every Island) sets a mana base back and is not this
tag: Omen of Fire is Wipe and LandDestruction, ruled 2026-09-28.

Read 2026-09-29, each shape tagged on every reviewed card using it: a target put ON TOP of its
owner's library (Time Ebb, Ether Well), SECOND or THIRD FROM THE TOP (Deem Inferior, Lost Days,
Riptide Gearhulk — a target, not the card itself as the God-Eternals do, nor a card from a hand as
Lost Hours does), "the OWNER OF TARGET … puts it on their choice of the top or bottom" (Riverwalk
Technique, Sudden Setback), the owner as the target (Hurkyl's Recall), and creatures the opponents
chose (Summon: Valefor). Not this, the same day: a permanent YOU CONTROL returned to hand (3 of 31
reviewed cards tagged — Clement, Hallowed Ground, Snow Hound, taken to the human; saving one in
response is Protection's), a single LAND (0 of 5 — Active Volcano, Flash Flood), the Auras on your
own permanent (Scarab of the Unseen), and a card exiled WITH the card coming back (Nyla). A clause
that names one of theirs beside yours still bounces theirs (Aether Tradewinds).

Ruled the same day: AIRBEND where an opponent's permanent can be is this tag (Airbending Lesson,
Aang, the Last Airbender, Avatar's Wrath), airbending your OWN is Protection (Airbender's Reversal,
Appa, Steadfast Guardian); the three reviewed cards returning your own permanent were slips
(Clement, Hallowed Ground, Snow Hound); an Aura's creature returned is this tag however it is paid
(Phantom Wings, Sun Clasp); a permanent exiled until the card leaves or dies and then returned to
its owner's hand is this tag, and Regrowth for the graveyard cards exiled with it (Aurelia's
Vindicator, The Spot); Fleeting Effigy's end-step return is Burn like the Viashinos, and Krile
Baldesion's tag was a misclick for Regrowth.

Read 2026-10-27: the other word order of Extraordinary Journey, "For as long as that card remains
exiled, its owner may play it", on "up to one other target tapped creature or Vehicle" (Prowl,
Stoic Strategist).
