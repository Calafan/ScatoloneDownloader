# Tokens

Canonical record of the rulings for `CardEffect.Tokens`. The one-line tooltip shown in the tagger is
in [`EffectGlossary.cs`](../../ScatoloneDownloader/Mtg/EffectGlossary.cs).

Narrowed 2026-09-15, and WIDENED the same day in the other direction: a body need not arrive as a
token at all, so earthbend and "all lands are 1/1 creatures" (Nature's Revolt) count — a go-wide
deck cares about the creatures, not the wording that made them.

Reaffirmed and widened 2026-09-19. Earthbend was found split 21 to 14 across the hand tags on the
IDENTICAL wording — and not one of the 21 tagged cards makes a real token, so the split was an
inconsistency rather than a distinction, and the 2026-09-15 reading stands. Four more shapes were
added the same day, all of them a body that never says "token" where a rule can read it.

FOUR KEYWORDS: cloak and manifest dread turn a card face down as a 2/2, living weapon and job select
hand the Equipment a Germ or a Hero to carry it; 29 reviewed cards carry one and 24 were already
tagged.

Plain MANIFEST was left out here, being one card and untagged; ruled in on 2026-10-15, below.

A TOKEN COPY counts only when what it copies is a CREATURE — Esoteric Duplicator copies an artifact
and Firion an Equipment, and neither puts anything on the board to attack with — asked of the LINE
that makes the copy, because the type word that answers it sits in the trigger beside the verb.

A NAMED token whose creature type lives only in its reminder text ("Create a Spellgorger Weird
token. (It's a {2}{R} 2/2 Weird creature…)") never says "creature token" anywhere. And the same
sentence with the verb at the END, which no rule could read: Stridehangar Automaton says "those
tokens plus an additional 1/1 Thopter … ARE CREATED instead".

NARROWED FOR CREATURES 2026-09-24, the largest cut this tag has taken: a NONCREATURE card that makes
a creature body is Tokens for a single one, while a CREATURE card is Tokens only when it makes THREE
OR MORE at once or the effect REPEATS. The reason is how the cube is filed — a spell that makes
bodies is counted among the creatures, and a creature already is one, so one Ally on the way in adds
nothing it was not already counted for. 81 reviewed creatures lost the tag by the rule and 9 cards
gained it; five more were slips found on the way (two printed token cards that make nothing, and a
Treasure, a Clue and a proliferate). It also settled a split nobody had ruled: the identical Ally
sentence was tagged on Katara and Invasion Reinforcements and not on Kyoshi Warriors or Treetop
Freedom Fighters.

Read per ability and WITH reminder text, where offspring, afterlife, fabricate, mobilize, myriad,
squad and encore keep their bodies; a keyword is counted outside its reminder, which names it again
(Dai Li Agents earthbends twice, not three times). X, "that many" and "for each" can always reach
three and keep the tag, and so does a LIST ("a 2/2 Wolf, a 3/3 Beast and a 4/4 Beast"); a DELAYED
trigger ("at the beginning of the next end step", Rukh Egg) and an ability that pays with the card
itself (Leering Onlooker exiles itself from the graveyard) run once.

The FRONT face is asked, so a Saga or Sidequest that transforms into a creature is still a
noncreature card.

A NONCREATURE permanent turned into a 0/0 creature and sized with counters is a body made, the same
shape as earthbend, ruled 2026-09-25 on Case of the Filched Falcon ("put four +1/+1 counters on
target noncreature artifact. It becomes a 0/0 Bird creature"). Two disagreements are left on
purpose: Graywater's Fixer grants encore to every outlaw in the graveyard, which repeats but reads
as one copy, and Siege-Gang Lieutenant repeats only while you control a commander, which a cube
never has, and stays untagged by hand (by the rule too since 2026-10-15, below).

A COPY of a creature SPELL becomes a token and counts, ruled 2026-09-28 ("copia una magia creature è
Token"): Case of the Shifting Visage, Double Down, Thurid, Jackal — never saying "create", so read
on its own. Copying any other spell of yours is Redirect.

INCUBATE is a body too, ruled 2026-09-29 ("solo da magia/ingresso"): Eyes of Gitaxias tagged,
Furnace Gremlin's Incubator on death untagged — a creature making one body once, on death or on
entry, is already the 2026-09-24 rule above.

Ruled 2026-10-13: a LAND or ARTIFACT that ANIMATES ITSELF is a body (the Restless lands, Cavernous
Maw, Levitating Statue, Eye of Malcator — 13 of 14 reviewed lands; Mishra's Factory, Jade Statue,
Xanthic Statue and Chimeric Sphere realigned), an enchantment that does it was not in the ruling
(ruled in on 2026-10-15, below); and DASH makes an enters trigger repeat (Riders of Rohan).

Read the same day: AMASS printed bare (Barad-dûr, Gríma Wormtongue, Sauron, the Dark Lord — 25 of 32
reviewed amass cards are tagged, and one amass as a creature enters stays the few-bodies rule), an
incubate repeated "X times" or "five times" (Progenitor Exarch, Elesh Norn), cards put FACE DOWN as
2/2s out of a library or a fresh mill (Cybership, The Cyber-Controller; out of exile or a graveyard
they are reanimated, Death in Heaven, Missy), and a copied PERMANENT spell (Archmage of Echoes,
which is not CardAdvantage). Somebody else creating is blanked SENTENCE BY SENTENCE rather than
vetoing the card (Oath of the Grey Host, Eiganjo Uprising, Blink), and a "noncreature token" named
in passing makes nothing (Bloodroot Apothecary, The Third Doctor).

Ruled 2026-10-15, five questions, which took the tag from F1 96.9% (884 right, 16 false, 40 missed)
to 98.1% (908, 19, 16) on the reviewed cards: an ARTIFACT that ANOTHER card animates is a body like
a land — split 6 to 7 by hand on the same wording (Majestic Metamorphosis, Katsumasa, Tezzeret,
Betrayer of Flesh, Depthshaker Titan, Mightstone's Animation, Zoetic Glyph tagged; Behind the Mask,
Relic's Roar, Synthesizer Labship, The Mechanist, Aerial Artisan, Tough Cookie, Xenic Poltergeist
and Unctus's Retrofitter not, the last still out as a creature animating once on entry), while an
artifact that becomes a COPY of something is not (True Polymorph); an ENCHANTMENT that animates
ITSELF counts like a land or an artifact (Emergent Haunting, Reverberating Summons, Case of the
Gorgon's Kiss); plain MANIFEST counts like cloak, under the creature rule (Guardian of the
Forgotten) — but not out of a graveyard (Ghastly Conscription) or when somebody else manifests; a
LIEUTENANT ability never counts, for ANY tag, being the commander clause again (Ironwill Forger
loses Tokens, Loyal Drake and Stormsurge Kraken CardAdvantage); a token copy of a NONCREATURE stays
out (Adagia, Windswept Bastion and Terra, Magical Adept).

Read the same day: lands that ARE or become X/X creatures (Jolrael, Voice of Zhalfir, Sage of the
Maze — 11 of 13 reviewed), "each player creates" a creature token makes one for you too (4 of 4, The
War Games), "that creature's controller creates" after a destroy or exile is the victim's (0 of 14),
a token copy of what an Aura or Equipment is attached to (Arna, Three Dog), a storm creature whose
copies become tokens (Stormscale Scion), a land cast face down by disguise or morph, a named token
whose type follows a quoted ability (Twisted Sewer-Witch), and "of those tokens" doubling a count
(Adipose Offspring).

Left as disagreements on purpose: Graywater's Fixer, Writhing Chrysalis (ruled 2026-09-29), Siege
Veteran (a tribal trigger, 4 to 2), Chandra, Spark Hunter (a Vehicle token, one card), Niko (Shards
become copies), Random Encounter, Tribute to Horobi. A Vehicle is not a body it makes: 17 of 108
reviewed Vehicles are tagged, and the rules read a body on 13 of them.

Ruled 2026-10-24, four questions. Any NONCREATURE card cast face down by morph or disguise is a 2/2
body, like the land of 2026-10-15 ("Tokens, come le terre"): Gift of Doom tagged, Concealed Weapon
and True Identity realigned and handed back. A token copy of a nonland PERMANENT CARD out of a
graveyard asks for no creature and is no body ("No Tokens se non chiede creature"): Tamiyo,
Compleated Sage, untagged by hand, while Nahiri's "creature or Equipment card" keeps the tag; a
copy of a permanent on the battlefield is still a body, which "permanent" alone would have taken
from Doppelgang, Extravagant Replication and Moonlit Meditation. Weatherlight Compleated, a Vehicle
made a creature by its own counters, was a slip: 19 reviewed Vehicles that animate themselves
other than by crew are untagged, War Balloon on the same sentence. Sicarian Infiltrator's squad
was a slip against the six other squad cards.

Read the same day: "you AND that player each create" makes one for you too, like "each player"
(Master of Ceremonies, tagged; Benevolent Offering, Sylvan Offering), and the veto written for "you
and target opponent each create" goes, since all it reached were Food, Treasures and Powerstones
(Oath of the Grey Host, Bloodroot Apothecary, Thran Spider). The seven disagreements above stay.

Ruled 2026-10-27, three questions. ENCORE and UNEARTH bring a creature back, and the bodies of
every entry are counted together, three or more being Tokens ("Sì, se le due entrate fanno 3+"):
Broodmate Tyrant, tagged. The cube is played 2vs2, so encore makes a copy for each of two opponents
("encore vale doppio") and the card enters three times and dies three times, its copies sacrificed
at the end step: Exquisite Huntmaster's Elf on death is "1 + 2 token", tagged by name. The copies
themselves are no bodies ("le copie non contano"), which settles Graywater's Fixer, whose only
bodies are the copies it hands out: untagged and handed back. Unearth returns the card once and
exiles it, so only its entry counts (Royal Warden's two Necrons are four); warp casts it again once
too, and Knight Luminary's one Soldier twice stays out. A body the exiled creature's controller gets
is not counted (Amphin Mutineer). A Vehicle TOKEN needs crew like a Vehicle card ("No, serve crew
come le carte"): Pia Nalaar, Chief Mechanic and Chandra, Spark Hunter untagged by name, Lita,
Mechanical Engineer already was.

Read the same day: "cloak THOSE CARDS", a pile with two library cards in it (Become Anonymous), and
a Tarmogoyf token printed with no reminder (Disa the Restless), the one named token in the store
that says no type anywhere. Left as disagreements on purpose: Writhing Chrysalis, Siege Veteran,
Niko, Random Encounter, Tribute to Horobi.

Ruled the same day, on those five. A body made on a TRIBE's trigger counts only when the card sets
the trigger off by itself ("i tribali contano solo se generano da soli"): a trigger on ANOTHER or a
NONTOKEN member, and the cast of a tribe's SPELL, since the tokens are no spells, are not Tokens.
Siege Veteran stays untagged, and eight cards tagged by hand are untagged and handed back: Baboon
Spirit, Bishop of Wings, Deeproot Pilgrimage, Knightfisher, Miirym, Rin and Seri, Rohgahh and Urtet.
A card of the tribe that says "a" or "one or more" (General Kreat, Zeriam, Crimson Cowl) and one
that makes the tribe's tokens itself (Origin of the Hidden Ones, Elvish Warmaster) keep the tag; a
land type is no tribe (Hazezon's Deserts, Titania's Forests). Writhing Chrysalis and Niko were slips
("Errore mio") and Tribute to Horobi's Rats are no Tokens either ("Niente"): each untagged by name.
Random Encounter is Burn and CardAdvantage instead ("Burn e CA", see Burn.md), untagged by name.
No disagreement is left on purpose.
