# Tutor

Canonical record of the rulings for `CardEffect.Tutor`. The one-line tooltip shown in the tagger is
in [`EffectGlossary.cs`](../../ScatoloneDownloader/Mtg/EffectGlossary.cs).

search library for a card (Demonic Tutor, Green Sun's Zenith, creature fetch) —
selection/consistency, not raw card advantage.

Widened 2026-09-19: the game names what it fetches in every way there is, and the old rule knew only
"a card" and five card TYPES.

A SUBTYPE (Equipment, Demon, Vehicle, Dragon), a COLOUR ("a black card, a green card, and a blue
card"), a COUNT ("three cards"), a NEGATION ("a nonland permanent card"), a second ZONE ("your
library and/or graveyard"), a search somebody else makes of their OWN library (Noble Benefactor),
and the sideboard, which the rules call "outside the game" (Ring of Ma'ruf). Two things are NOT this
tag.

A LAND search is Ramp or ManaFixing — the ruling the tag was written around, and it costs Elemental
Teachings, one card against the family; a land on one side of an "or" does not make the whole search
a land search (Starfield Shepherd).

REVEALING UNTIL a type turns up hands you whichever one was nearest the top rather than one you
chose — The Regalia, House Cartographer, Yuna's Whistle, Hei Bai and Sacred Guide are all untagged,
while Demonic Consultation digs for a NAME you picked and counts. Naming the card you want was
vetoed for half a day on the evidence that five reviewed cards said it and none was tagged; ruled
the other way the same day, because it is the purest form of the tag whether it finds another copy
of itself, a combo piece, or somebody else's payoff (Raven Clan War-Axe, Tower Winder), and the veto
was costing Dragonstorm Forecaster. Searching without putting the card in your HAND counts too —
Ugin exiles and casts them free, Goblin Recruiter stacks the top of the library, Foresight just
exiles. 26 wrong out of 5,037 reviewed cards down to 1, and that one is Elemental Teachings, held
out by the land ruling on purpose.

Read 2026-10-16: a search for "a creature or land card" (Analyze the Pollen; Traverse the Ulvenwald,
unreviewed). Handed back: Central Elevator, Thornvault Forager and The Hunger Tide Rises with the
tag; Urza's Cave without it.
