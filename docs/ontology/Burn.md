# Burn

Canonical record of the rulings for `CardEffect.Burn`. The one-line tooltip shown in the tagger is
in [`EffectGlossary.cs`](../../ScatoloneDownloader/Mtg/EffectGlossary.cs).

target" is both, because it can go either way. A sweeper is Burn only when it catches the players

Ruled 2026-09-15.

Seven more rulings on 2026-09-20, taken one family at a time from the 109 cards where hand and
classifier disagreed, and most of them were the classifier reading one wording out of five.

DAMAGE DIVIDED among bare "TARGETS" is Burn because a face can be one of them (Fireball, Rolling
Thunder, Pyrotechnics, Meteor Shower, Mogg Mob), while "divided among target CREATURES" never
reaches a player and is not (Pyrokinesis, Fire Covenant, Forked Lightning and four more). Fiery
Justice is the exception the human ruled ON PURPOSE: it deals five and hands the same opponent five
life back, so the count is nothing.

THE WORD ORDER RUNS BOTH WAYS — "deals damage TO that player EQUAL TO the number of artifacts they
control" as readily as "deals damage equal to … to that player" — and only one of the two was being
read.

THAT PERMANENT'S CONTROLLER is how the old punishers name a face (Psychic Venom, Ankh of Mishra,
Dingus Egg and Staff, Haunting Wind, Seizures, Artifact Possession, Stinging Licid), but the same
words hanging off an ANSWER are a removal spell with a bonus and are not this tag: Detonate,
Icequake, Cinder Cloud and Stench of Evil destroy first, Misthios's Fury and Wisecrack shoot the
creature first, and Judgment Bolt elides the second verb entirely.

LIFE LOSS COUNTS FROM TWO. One point is a rider: eleven reviewed cards drain exactly one and carry
no tag, against two that do, and one of those two (Susurian Voidborn) is word for word Al Bhed
Salvagers, which does not.

REVISED 2026-09-29 ("ripetuto sì, singolo no"): one point that comes AGAIN AND AGAIN — a trigger, an
activation, extort — IS this tag, because since 09-23 the human had tagged twelve of sixteen such
drains (Vengeful Bloodwitch, Raven of Fell Omens, Wispdrinker Vampire, Mirkwood Bats); one point
ONCE (a spell, an enters or dies trigger) stays the rider, and Al Bhed Salvagers, Susurian Voidborn
and the other repeated drains left untagged went back for review. A drain in the quotes of a token
the card makes is the token's (Keimi, Tatsunari's Frog). X counts, because X is not one, and so does
"one life FOR EACH" of something.

"LOSES LIFE EQUAL TO" splits on whose life it is — four cards say YOU lose it and none is tagged
(Reanimate, Lich, Teval, Darkstar Augur), seven say somebody else does and six are.

THE UPKEEP TAX IS BURN however slowly it burns, ruled after the hand tags came out ten to nine on
cards printing the same sentence (Karma against Ancient Runes, Power Surge against Storm World). And
LIFE PAID to stop a card is life lost (Breathstealer's Crypt, Sirocco, Cleansing), again from two,
unless it is charged for each of something. The one ruling that went the other way: WHAT A TOKEN
DOES IS THE TOKEN'S. Eight reviewed cards create the 0/1 Wizard that pings on every noncreature
spell and none of the eight carries Burn, so the card that makes a body earns Tokens and the body's
damage is the body's — keyed on "THIS TOKEN" inside the quotes, so an ability granted to a creature
you already control keeps the tag (Black Mage's Rod says "this creature"). Both of the leftovers
were ruled the same evening.

A CREATURE WITH HASTE THAT IS GONE AT THE END OF THE TURN it arrived is a burn spell with legs: it
attacks once and then is not there any more, so what it really did was put its power on a face. That
covers the ones that die (Ball Lightning, Spark Elemental, Blistering Firecat, Groundbreaker,
Lightning Skelemental) and the ones that BOUNCE (Viashino Sandstalker, Archwing Dragon,
Glitterfang), and it is asked of the card with QUOTED text blanked out, because Strago and Relm,
Skirk Alarmist and Apprentice Necromancer hand that drawback to somebody else's creature.

NB this is the one rule in the file the reviewed set cannot really test: Ball Lightning is tagged,
Viashino Sandstalker was not and is realigned by the ruling, and the other thirteen cards written
that way are unreviewed. And on a LAND-DESTROYER the damage is a second effect the human reads as
worth counting, which settles the split this family had — Orcish Mine charges the controller two for
the land it just destroyed and is tagged, Icequake and Stench of Evil do the same and were not. A
land is therefore not among the permanents whose death excuses the charge; a creature, an artifact,
a permanent and a spell still are. Inferno is not a classifier problem at all: the store entry is
keyed to a TEXTLESS theme card of the same name, and four more entries have the same wrong key
(Bounty Hunter, Drowned, Grave Robbers, Spider-Verse).

A drain on ANOTHER MEMBER OF A TRIBE is not this tag, ruled 2026-10-13 on Obyra, Dreaming Duelist
("Whenever another Faerie you control enters"): it waits on cards the card is not. A card that makes
that tribe itself (Aatchik's Insects, Boggart Mischief's Goblins), or is of it and fires on itself
("a Sliver", Leeching Sliver), keeps it; "another creature you control" is any body and keeps it too
(Thunder of Unity). A hasty TOKEN sacrificed or exiled at the next end step is the Viashino's burn
made by another card, ruled 2026-10-14 for the whole family ("tutta la famiglia Burn"): Urabrask's
Forge was tagged, Balduvian Dead and Daring Piracy were not and were handed back with it; a token
COPY given haste is not this (Kiki-Jiki) — overturned on 2026-10-23, below.

Read the same day: "each of UP TO TWO targets" (Chandra, Hope's Beacon), "another target battle or
opponent" (Invasion of Regatha), "its OWNER loses life equal to" for a spell cast from an opponent's
library (The Ruinous Powers), a relative clause opened by "who" or "dealt" up to 90 characters
(Gollum, Obsessed Stalker — widening every subject reached Scarring Memories, then untagged), EXCESS
damage handed on to the controller, which is not a charge for the kill (Gandalf's Sanction), and an
Aura handing its creature "unless you pay 2 life" every upkeep (Morgul-Knife Wound); Kaito, Dancing
Shadow's Drone keeps its drain to itself. Measured: Burn F1 98.1 to 98.6 before the store pass.

Ruled 2026-10-16 (B1, "Mai Burn"): a player who DRAWS N CARDS AND LOSES N LIFE is never burnt; the
life is what the cards cost, whoever pays it. Monumental Corruption was tagged and is corrected by
name; Ob Nixilis, the Adversary keeps the tag through its +1 drain, another sentence. A drain that
answers an OPPONENT'S SACRIFICE falls the same way, as the hand already had it (Vengeful Tracker
untagged). Both are dropped only when nothing else on the card reads as Burn once they are blanked.

Ruled 2026-10-16 (B2, "Sempre Burn"): an opponent, a target player or a defending player losing HALF
THEIR LIFE is always Burn. Alpharael, Stonechosen and Betor, Kin to All were untagged and are handed
back with it; "its owner" (Personal Incarnation) and "each player" (Shahrazad) aim at nobody and
stay out. Blooming Blast's 3 damage to the controller is no Burn (B3, "Nessun Burn", named): it
rides an answer, which is the Misthios's Fury rule.

Read the same day: damage to "each of your opponents" (Aurelia, the Law Above), a life gain answered
with "that much life" lost (Enduring Tenacity; the broad "loses that much life" measured 1 tagged of
3 and was narrowed to a life gain), a drain on entering by a card that returns itself to hand
(Intimidation Campaign) and damage divided among "up to eight targets" (Blaster Hulk). A WARD paid
in life ("counter it unless that player pays 2 life") taxes their spell: 0 of the 6 reviewed are
tagged. Arbiter of Woe, whose entering drain the rules already read, is handed back with the tag.

Ruled 2026-10-23 ("Sì, sempre Burn" for encore, then "Sì, tutto allineato"): HASTE AND GONE AT THE
NEXT END STEP is Burn whatever the mechanism. It holds for the card itself — encore, unearth, blitz
and dash, asked by keyword because Phyrexian Dragon Engine, Ziatora's Envoy and Broodmate Tyrant
print theirs without the reminder, and the same keywords handed to other cards (Graywater's Fixer,
Dregscape Sliver, Sedris, Henzie "Toolbox" Torre) — and for every token or COPY the card makes
(Kiki-Jiki, Hate Mirage, Lagomos, Chandra, Acolyte of Flame, the populate of Determined Iteration).
That overturns the Kiki-Jiki exclusion of 2026-10-14, which was a narrowing made in the code rather
than a ruling. A creature the card only TAKES — from a graveyard, a hand, a library or an opponent
(Corpse Dance, Shallow Grave, Through the Breach, Zirilan, Slave of Bolas) — is not burn and keeps
its Reanimate, Cheat or Steal ("No, solo ciò che la carta crea o è"); nor is unearth on an artifact
(Mask of the Jadecrafter). Measured: 17 reviewed cards tagged by hand are now read (the thirteen
encore of 2026-10-20, Hate Mirage, Araumi, Dregscape Sliver, First-Sphere Gargantua), and 59
untagged ones are reached: Amphin Mutineer, Briarblade Adept, Graywater's Fixer and Mist Dancer,
named in the question, are corrected, and the other 55 are handed back.

Ruled the same day ("Sì, Burn"): a life loss at the END OF A LIST of what the opponent does —
"sacrifices a creature of their choice, discards a card, and loses 4 life" — is Burn. Dusk Mangler
was tagged; Scarring Memories was not and is corrected by name; Mind Drain's one point stays under
the threshold. Unleash Shell's 2 damage to the controller of what it kills was a slip ("Svista"),
the B3 rule, and is corrected by name. Every printed token, dungeon and emblem is untagged for every
tag (`CardEffect`), which also takes the proposals off The Ring and Tomb of Annihilation.

Read the same day: "that SOURCE'S CONTROLLER loses that much life unless they sacrifice" (Archfiend
of Spite), damage EQUAL TO a count divided among any number of targets (Orca, Siege Demon), a "who"
clause before "equal to" (Spiteful Repossession), an EMBLEM handed to an opponent (Chandra, Awakened
Inferno — an emblem you get stays yours, Ajani Steadfast), an upkeep drain handed to an enchanted
permanent (Clawing Torment), "creatures, planeswalkers, and/or players" (Soulfire Eruption) and
"target creature token, player, or planeswalker" (Coalborn Entity). The shield that counters a
spell "unless that player pays 4 life" is Parnesse, the Subtle Brush's Protection, as ward is.
Left as known disagreements, tagged by hand on wordings no other card shares: Blim, Comedic Genius
(life lost per permanent controlled but not owned) and Yurlok of Scorch Thrash (unspent mana).
