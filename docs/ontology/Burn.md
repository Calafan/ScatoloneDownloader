# Burn

Canonical record of the rulings for `CardEffect.Burn`. The one-line tooltip shown in the tagger is
in [`EffectGlossary.cs`](../../ScatoloneDownloader/Mtg/EffectGlossary.cs).

An OPPONENT loses life to what the card does: damage that can land on their face, life they lose,
or life they pay to stop the card; two or more, or one point that comes again and again. Lightning
Bolt is the card. The rest follows from the sentence: damage only a creature can take is Removal,
and "any target" is both; the life YOU lose or pay is a price (Ancient Tomb, Reanimate); one point
once is a rider (Mind Drain); a creature with haste gone at the end of the turn is the damage
itself, wherever it came from (Ball Lightning, Through the Breach, Shallow Grave); the charge on the
controller of what the card just destroyed is a rider on the kill (Detonate, Choking Sands). Four
exceptions are kept, each with its why. Life that BUYS something for whoever pays it is a price: a
fixed "target player draws N cards and loses N life" (Blood Pact) and a ward paid in life, the toll
on their own spell (Zul Ashur, Parnesse), though a variable X pointed at a player is a kill
(Eldritch Pact). What a TOKEN the card makes then does is the token's (the 0/1 Wizard), unless the
token is itself the hasty shot (Urabrask's Forge). A drain only ANOTHER MEMBER OF A TRIBE turns on,
or a hasty creature card of one TYPE put in and gone, needs a tribal deck (Obyra, Incandescent
Soulstoke), the one exception Reanimate keeps too. And a card that hands back the
life it deals is worth nothing (Fiery Justice, read by its shape). Redefined 2026-11-06, the
answers at the end; every earlier paragraph marked "overturned" is history, and no card is kept in
or out of this tag by name.

Direct damage at a FACE (Lightning Bolt, Fireblast, Price of Progress). Damage pointed at a creature
is how the game kills creatures and is already Removal, so Explosive Shot is Removal alone; "any
target" is both, because it can go either way. A sweeper is Burn only when it catches the players
too — Inferno's "each creature and each player" is Wipe AND Burn, Pyroclasm is Wipe alone. Damage to
YOURSELF (Ancient Tomb, City of Brass, Juzam Djinn) is a price the card charges, not an effect.

Ruled 2026-09-15.

Seven more rulings on 2026-09-20, taken one family at a time from the 109 cards where hand and
classifier disagreed, and most of them were the classifier reading one wording out of five.

DAMAGE DIVIDED among bare "TARGETS" is Burn because a face can be one of them (Fireball, Rolling
Thunder, Pyrotechnics, Meteor Shower, Mogg Mob), while "divided among target CREATURES" never
reaches a player and is not (Pyrokinesis, Fire Covenant, Forked Lightning and four more). Fiery
Justice is the exception the human ruled ON PURPOSE: it deals five and hands the same opponent five
life back, so the count is nothing (a shape since 2026-11-06, Q8 below, not a name).

THE WORD ORDER RUNS BOTH WAYS — "deals damage TO that player EQUAL TO the number of artifacts they
control" as readily as "deals damage equal to … to that player" — and only one of the two was being
read.

THAT PERMANENT'S CONTROLLER is how the old punishers name a face (Psychic Venom, Ankh of Mishra,
Dingus Egg and Staff, Haunting Wind, Seizures, Artifact Possession, Stinging Licid), but the same
words hanging off an ANSWER are a removal spell with a bonus and are not this tag: Detonate,
Icequake, Cinder Cloud and Stench of Evil destroy first, Misthios's Fury and Wisecrack shoot the
creature first, and Judgment Bolt elides the second verb entirely.

LIFE LOSS COUNTS FROM TWO. One point is a rider: eleven reviewed cards drain exactly one and carry
no tag, against two that do, and one of those two (Susurian Voidborn) is word for word Al Bhed
Salvagers, which does not.

REVISED 2026-09-29 ("ripetuto sì, singolo no"): one point that comes AGAIN AND AGAIN — a trigger, an
activation, extort — IS this tag, because since 09-23 the human had tagged twelve of sixteen such
drains (Vengeful Bloodwitch, Raven of Fell Omens, Wispdrinker Vampire, Mirkwood Bats); one point
ONCE (a spell, an enters or dies trigger) stays the rider, and Al Bhed Salvagers, Susurian Voidborn
and the other repeated drains left untagged went back for review. A drain in the quotes of a token
the card makes is the token's (Keimi, Tatsunari's Frog). X counts, because X is not one, and so does
"one life FOR EACH" of something.

"LOSES LIFE EQUAL TO" splits on whose life it is — four cards say YOU lose it and none is tagged
(Reanimate, Lich, Teval, Darkstar Augur), seven say somebody else does and six are.

THE UPKEEP TAX IS BURN however slowly it burns, ruled after the hand tags came out ten to nine on
cards printing the same sentence (Karma against Ancient Runes, Power Surge against Storm World). And
LIFE PAID to stop a card is life lost (Breathstealer's Crypt, Sirocco, Cleansing), again from two,
unless it is charged for each of something. The one ruling that went the other way: WHAT A TOKEN
DOES IS THE TOKEN'S. Eight reviewed cards create the 0/1 Wizard that pings on every noncreature
spell and none of the eight carries Burn, so the card that makes a body earns Tokens and the body's
damage is the body's — keyed on "THIS TOKEN" inside the quotes, so an ability granted to a creature
you already control keeps the tag (Black Mage's Rod says "this creature"). Both of the leftovers
were ruled the same evening.

A CREATURE WITH HASTE THAT IS GONE AT THE END OF THE TURN it arrived is a burn spell with legs: it
attacks once and then is not there any more, so what it really did was put its power on a face. That
covers the ones that die (Ball Lightning, Spark Elemental, Blistering Firecat, Groundbreaker,
Lightning Skelemental) and the ones that BOUNCE (Viashino Sandstalker, Archwing Dragon,
Glitterfang), and it is asked of the card with QUOTED text blanked out, because Strago and Relm,
Skirk Alarmist and Apprentice Necromancer hand that drawback to somebody else's creature.

NB this is the one rule in the file the reviewed set cannot really test: Ball Lightning is tagged,
Viashino Sandstalker was not and is realigned by the ruling, and the other thirteen cards written
that way are unreviewed. And on a LAND-DESTROYER the damage is a second effect the human reads as
worth counting (OVERTURNED 2026-11-06, Q1 below: a rider for a land too), which settles the split
this family had — Orcish Mine charges the controller two for
the land it just destroyed and is tagged, Icequake and Stench of Evil do the same and were not. A
land is therefore not among the permanents whose death excuses the charge; a creature, an artifact,
a permanent and a spell still are. Inferno is not a classifier problem at all: the store entry is
keyed to a TEXTLESS theme card of the same name, and four more entries have the same wrong key
(Bounty Hunter, Drowned, Grave Robbers, Spider-Verse).

A drain on ANOTHER MEMBER OF A TRIBE is not this tag, ruled 2026-10-13 on Obyra, Dreaming Duelist
("Whenever another Faerie you control enters"): it waits on cards the card is not. A card that makes
that tribe itself (Aatchik's Insects, Boggart Mischief's Goblins), or is of it and fires on itself
("a Sliver", Leeching Sliver), keeps it; "another creature you control" is any body and keeps it too
(Thunder of Unity). A hasty TOKEN sacrificed or exiled at the next end step is the Viashino's burn
made by another card, ruled 2026-10-14 for the whole family ("tutta la famiglia Burn"): Urabrask's
Forge was tagged, Balduvian Dead and Daring Piracy were not and were handed back with it; a token
COPY given haste is not this (Kiki-Jiki) — overturned on 2026-10-23, below.

Read the same day: "each of UP TO TWO targets" (Chandra, Hope's Beacon), "another target battle or
opponent" (Invasion of Regatha), "its OWNER loses life equal to" for a spell cast from an opponent's
library (The Ruinous Powers), a relative clause opened by "who" or "dealt" up to 90 characters
(Gollum, Obsessed Stalker — widening every subject reached Scarring Memories, then untagged), EXCESS
damage handed on to the controller, which is not a charge for the kill (Gandalf's Sanction), and an
Aura handing its creature "unless you pay 2 life" every upkeep (Morgul-Knife Wound); Kaito, Dancing
Shadow's Drone keeps its drain to itself. Measured: Burn F1 98.1 to 98.6 before the store pass.

Ruled 2026-10-16 (B1, "Mai Burn"): a player who DRAWS N CARDS AND LOSES N LIFE is never burnt
(except a VARIABLE X pointed at a player, RULED 2026-11-05 below); the life is what the cards cost,
whoever pays it. Monumental Corruption was tagged and is corrected by name; Ob Nixilis, the
Adversary keeps the tag through its +1 drain, another sentence. A drain that answers an OPPONENT'S
SACRIFICE falls the same way, as the hand already had it (Vengeful Tracker untagged; OVERTURNED
2026-11-06, Q4 below: Burn like every punisher). Both are
dropped only when nothing else on the card reads as Burn once they are blanked.

Ruled 2026-10-16 (B2, "Sempre Burn"): an opponent, a target player or a defending player losing HALF
THEIR LIFE is always Burn. Alpharael, Stonechosen and Betor, Kin to All were untagged and are handed
back with it; "its owner" (Personal Incarnation) and "each player" (Shahrazad) aim at nobody and
stay out. Blooming Blast's 3 damage to the controller is no Burn (B3, "Nessun Burn", named): it
rides an answer, which is the Misthios's Fury rule.

Read the same day: damage to "each of your opponents" (Aurelia, the Law Above), a life gain answered
with "that much life" lost (Enduring Tenacity; the broad "loses that much life" measured 1 tagged of
3 and was narrowed to a life gain), a drain on entering by a card that returns itself to hand
(Intimidation Campaign) and damage divided among "up to eight targets" (Blaster Hulk). A WARD paid
in life ("counter it unless that player pays 2 life") taxes their spell: 0 of the 6 reviewed are
tagged. Arbiter of Woe, whose entering drain the rules already read, is handed back with the tag.

Ruled 2026-10-23 ("Sì, sempre Burn" for encore, then "Sì, tutto allineato"): HASTE AND GONE AT THE
NEXT END STEP is Burn whatever the mechanism. It holds for the card itself — encore, unearth, blitz
and dash, asked by keyword because Phyrexian Dragon Engine, Ziatora's Envoy and Broodmate Tyrant
print theirs without the reminder, and the same keywords handed to other cards (Graywater's Fixer,
Dregscape Sliver, Sedris, Henzie "Toolbox" Torre) — and for every token or COPY the card makes
(Kiki-Jiki, Hate Mirage, Lagomos, Chandra, Acolyte of Flame, the populate of Determined Iteration).
That overturns the Kiki-Jiki exclusion of 2026-10-14, which was a narrowing made in the code rather
than a ruling. A creature the card only TAKES — from a graveyard, a hand, a library or an opponent
(Corpse Dance, Shallow Grave, Through the Breach, Zirilan, Slave of Bolas) — is not burn and keeps
its Reanimate, Cheat or Steal ("No, solo ciò che la carta crea o è"; the library part overturned
2026-10-27, the graveyard part 2026-11-05 and the hand 2026-11-06, below; the opponent's creature
is Removal since 2026-11-06); nor is unearth on an artifact (Mask of the
Jadecrafter). Measured: 17 reviewed cards tagged by hand are now read (the thirteen encore of
2026-10-20, Hate Mirage, Araumi, Dregscape Sliver, First-Sphere Gargantua), and 59 untagged ones are
reached: Amphin Mutineer, Briarblade Adept, Graywater's Fixer and Mist Dancer, named in the
question, are corrected, and the other 55 are handed back.

Ruled the same day ("Sì, Burn"): a life loss at the END OF A LIST of what the opponent does —
"sacrifices a creature of their choice, discards a card, and loses 4 life" — is Burn. Dusk Mangler
was tagged; Scarring Memories was not and is corrected by name; Mind Drain's one point stays under
the threshold. Unleash Shell's 2 damage to the controller of what it kills was a slip ("Svista"),
the B3 rule, and is corrected by name. Every printed token, dungeon and emblem is untagged for every
tag (`CardEffect`), which also takes the proposals off The Ring and Tomb of Annihilation.

Read the same day: "that SOURCE'S CONTROLLER loses that much life unless they sacrifice" (Archfiend
of Spite), damage EQUAL TO a count divided among any number of targets (Orca, Siege Demon), a "who"
clause before "equal to" (Spiteful Repossession), an EMBLEM handed to an opponent (Chandra, Awakened
Inferno — an emblem you get stays yours, Ajani Steadfast), an upkeep drain handed to an enchanted
permanent (Clawing Torment), "creatures, planeswalkers, and/or players" (Soulfire Eruption) and
"target creature token, player, or planeswalker" (Coalborn Entity). The shield that counters a
spell "unless that player pays 4 life" is Parnesse, the Subtle Brush's Protection, as ward is.
Left as known disagreements, tagged by hand on wordings no other card shares: Blim, Comedic Genius
(life lost per permanent controlled but not owned) and Yurlok of Scorch Thrash (unspent mana).

Ruled 2026-10-27 ("Allarga la regola alla libreria", then "Sì, tutte"): a creature put onto the
battlefield FROM THE LIBRARY with haste and gone at the next end step is Burn, whether it was milled
(Random Encounter, tagged by name with CardAdvantage), exiled from the top (Anzrag's Rampage) or
searched for (Zirilan of the Claw). That overturns the library part of the 2026-10-23 exclusion; a
creature taken from a graveyard (overturned 2026-11-05, below), a hand or an opponent still is not
burn. Anzrag's Rampage and Zirilan were untagged and are handed back with the tag. Random
Encounter's CardAdvantage is not read by the rules, which read no card advantage in a creature
returned to hand at the end step (Anzrag's Rampage, same wording, is untagged): a known
disagreement.

Read 2026-11-05 under the 2026-09-20 ruling (damage divided among bare targets): "divided as you
choose among ONE OR TWO targets" (Ral, Monsoon Mage, Burn by hand; Forked Bolt, Electrolyze, Arc
Mage, Chandra's Pyrohelix, Twin Bolt, Skarrgan Hellkite, Fire // Ice, Samut, the Tested and Death to
Our Enemies, unreviewed, gain the tag). A player in an "each of up to" list of separate targets is
the same reading as Coalborn Entity and Soulfire Eruption (Volatile Arsonist, Burn by hand). The
player a card CHOSE earlier is a player the damage lands on (The Lord of Pain, Burn by hand; Saskia
the Unyielding and Stuffy Doll, unreviewed, gain the tag); Sawhorn Nemesis only doubles damage dealt
to the chosen player and stays out.

Named 2026-11-05, a slip: Dorothea, Vengeful Victim, whose Aura makes a 4/4 Spirit "tapped and
attacking" and sacrifices it at end of combat, is not Burn (1 tagged of 13 reviewed cards with that
wording; the rules already read Tokens alone). Read by its shape since 2026-11-06, Q3 below.

RULED 2026-11-05 ("Sì: target player + X = Burn", asked on Eldritch Pact against Pact of the Serpent
and Monumental Corruption): "TARGET PLAYER draws X cards and loses X life" with a VARIABLE X is
Burn, because X can be large and the player can be an opponent. B1 (2026-10-16) still holds for a
fixed N pointed at a player (Sign in Blood, Blood Pact, Insatiable Avarice, Vault Plunderer) and for
YOU drawing X and losing X (Estinien Varlineau, The Speed Demon, Painful Truths). Eldritch Pact is
named and stays Burn. Pact of the Serpent (11-05) and Monumental Corruption (10-09, tagged without
Burn under B1) are reached and handed back with the tag; Damnable Pact is the unreviewed one that
follows. Family measured: 4 cards say it, against 5 that say "you draw X cards and lose X life".

RULED 2026-11-05 ("Anche preso è Burn", asked on Balduvian Atrocity against Shallow Grave and Corpse
Dance): a creature card returned FROM A GRAVEYARD to the battlefield with haste and sacrificed or
exiled at the next end step is Burn, beside its Reanimate. That OVERTURNS the graveyard part of the
2026-10-23 exclusion ("solo ciò che la carta crea o è"), as the library part was overturned on
2026-10-27. What stands of 2026-10-23 (the HAND and the return to HAND OVERTURNED 2026-11-06, Q2
below): a creature taken from a HAND (Through the Breach) or from an
opponent's battlefield (Slave of Bolas) is not burn; unearth on an artifact is not (Mask of the
Jadecrafter); and neither is a card that is no CREATURE card (Kami of Industry's artifact, Moira and
Teshar's nonland permanent: read by the wording, a type-blind rule would reach exactly those two)
nor a return to HAND at the end step (Swift Warkite, from hand or graveyard). Balduvian Atrocity is
named and stays Burn. Reached and handed back with the tag: Corpse Dance, Shallow Grave, Push //
Pull and Coalstoke Gearhulk (all Reanimate by hand). Unreviewed, gaining the tag: Apprentice
Necromancer, Dawn of the Dead, Goryo's Vengeance, Gruesome Encore, Macabre Mockery, Postmortem
Lunge, Puppeteer Clique, Whip of Erebos.

REDEFINED 2026-11-06 from Lightning Bolt ("Sì, questa"), the sentence at the top of this file, then
eight answers taken one at a time against it.

Q1 ("Mai Burn, terre comprese", asked on Detonate against Choking Sands): the charge on the
controller of what the card just destroyed is a rider on the kill whatever was destroyed. 12 of 12
reviewed removal riders were untagged (Detonate, Judgment Bolt, Rending Flame, Burn the Accursed),
4 of 4 land-destroyer riders tagged. That OVERTURNS the land part of 2026-09-20: Choking Sands,
Icequake (one point once, under the threshold anyway), Stench of Evil and Orcish Mine are handed
back without the tag, and seven unreviewed land destroyers lose the proposal (Cryoclasm, Melt
Terrain, Molten Rain, Peak Eruption, Poison the Well, Roiling Terrain, Steam Vines).

Q2 (the second option, then "Steal + Removal"): a creature with haste gone at the end step is Burn
WHEREVER IT CAME FROM — a hand (Through the Breach, Sneak Attack, Terminal Velocity, whose
sacrifice is in the quotes it grants), the command zone (Hellkite Courser) — and whether it dies,
is exiled or goes back to a hand or the command zone (Swift Warkite, as Anzrag's Rampage already
was). That OVERTURNS what stood of 2026-10-23. A creature STOLEN and sacrificed at the next end
step (Slave of Bolas, In Thrall to the Pit kicked) is no Burn: its attack is Act of Treason's,
which nobody calls burn; what the sacrifice adds is the creature gone from its owner for good,
which is Removal beside the Steal. A card that is no creature card stays out by the words (Arms
Race, Kami of Industry, Moira and Teshar). Handed back: Terminal Velocity and Hellkite Courser with
Burn, In Thrall to the Pit with Removal. Unreviewed, gaining Burn: Through the Breach, Sneak
Attack, Swift Warkite, Cauldron Dance, Incandescent Soulstoke, Meek Attack, Shifty Doppelganger;
gaining Removal: Slave of Bolas, Angrath, the Flame-Chained.

Q3 ("Non Burn, Arthur -Burn"): a body an attack trigger puts onto the battlefield TAPPED AND
ATTACKING and takes away at end of combat joins an attack already declared. It is an extra
attacker (Tokens), not the shot with legs, which has haste and attacks on its own. 1 of the 6
reviewed cards was tagged: Arthur, Marigold Knight, named, loses Burn; Kavaron Harrier, Altaïr,
Mirror-Style Master, Mordor Trebuchet and Dorothea's Retribution were already untagged.

Q4 ("Burn, veto tolto"): a punisher on an opponent's own SACRIFICE is Burn, as every punisher is
(Ankh of Mishra, Psychic Venom). The veto of 2026-10-16 said 0 of 3, but two of the three drained
nobody (Legate Lanius, Malik). Vengeful Tracker, named, gains Burn, and Nazgûl Battle-Mace's
"unless that player pays 3 life", tagged by hand, is read again.

Q5a ("Prezzo delle carte: fuori"): B1 stands for a fixed N, because whoever pays the life gets the
cards; four reviewed cards say it at two or more and none is tagged (Blood Pact, Fell Stinger,
Unscrupulous Contractor, Insatiable Avarice; Vault Plunderer and Silverquill Command drain one and
fall to the threshold). The variable X of 2026-11-05 stays Burn: Eldritch Pact on a full graveyard
is a kill, not a trade. Q5b ("Ward fuori, col perché"): life paid to STOP THE CARD is Burn, because
the card acts on them (Sirocco, Breathstealer's Crypt, Giant Albatross, Nazgûl Battle-Mace, Sivriss:
5 of 5 tagged); life paid for their OWN action against your permanents is its toll, Protection, and
they can avoid it by not targeting (ward, 10 of 10 untagged for that line; Parnesse).

Q6 ("Del token: fuori"): what a token the card makes does afterwards is the token's (21 of 21
reviewed untagged for that line); the hasty token gone at the end step stays Burn because there the
body is the one shot. Q7 ("Tribale fuori, come Reanimate"): the drain on another member of a tribe
stays out with its why, and so does the hasty creature card of one type, ruled after the pass
("Incandescent Soulstoke niente Burn, regola tribale anche qui"): its Elemental card from the hand
is fed only by a tribal deck. It was the one card of the store the Q2 rule reached that way; the
keyword a Sliver hands its own tribe stays Burn (Dregscape Sliver, Sliver Gravemother), because
the card is of the tribe and has it too. Q8 ("Fuori: il saldo è zero"): Fiery Justice's damage
handed back as life is read by its shape, damage then "target opponent gains N life".

Left as known disagreements, each the only card with its wording: Blim, Comedic Genius, Yurlok of
Scorch Thrash, and Deadpool, Trading Card, whose "you lose 3 life" reaches an opponent only through
the exchange of text boxes.
