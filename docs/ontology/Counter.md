# Counter

Canonical record of the rulings for `CardEffect.Counter`. The one-line tooltip shown in the tagger
is in [`EffectGlossary.cs`](../../ScatoloneDownloader/Mtg/EffectGlossary.cs).

answer a spell on the stack by countering it.

Read 2026-10-13 from the 10-06..10-11 sitting: an activated or triggered ability countered is the
same answer ("Sì, tutti Counter": Tishana's Tidebinder, Brown Ouphe, Rust, Adric, Ayesha Tanaka); so
is a spell RETURNED to its owner's hand (Reprieve, Spellscorn Coven — not Bounce unless a permanent
can be the target too), exiled (Smirking Spelljacker, Ertai's Meddling), put into its owner's
library (Sudden Setback), airbent (Aang, Swift Savior), or chosen and countered further on (Aether
Spike). Not one of YOUR OWN spells (Goblin Artisans), not one that targets the card itself
(Mistfolk), and not the counterspell a created token carries in its quotes (Mage's Attendant).
