# Counter

Canonical record of the rulings for `CardEffect.Counter`. The one-line tooltip shown in the tagger
is in [`EffectGlossary.cs`](../../ScatoloneDownloader/Mtg/EffectGlossary.cs).

Somebody else's SPELL OR ABILITY taken off the stack before it resolves — countered, exiled, returned
to its owner's hand or put into the library — by an answer YOU time: a target, or a moment (flash,
entering, turned face up). Counterspell is the card. A trigger that counters by itself whatever is
cast is a rule of the table, like ward, and not this tag. The rest follows from the sentence: one of
your own spells is nobody's answer (Goblin Artisans, Krark), and an instant that ends the turn takes
their spell off the stack (Hurkyl's Final Meditation). Two shapes stay out, each with its why: a
counter that protects ONLY the card itself (Mistfolk: the self rule every tag keeps), and the
counterspell a created token carries in its quotes (Mage's Attendant: what a token does is the
token's, as for Burn). Defined 2026-11-08, the rulings below; no card is kept in or out by name.

Read 2026-10-13 from the 10-06..10-11 sitting: an activated or triggered ability countered is the
same answer ("Sì, tutti Counter": Tishana's Tidebinder, Brown Ouphe, Rust, Adric, Ayesha Tanaka); so
is a spell RETURNED to its owner's hand (Reprieve, Spellscorn Coven — not Bounce unless a permanent
can be the target too), exiled (Smirking Spelljacker, Ertai's Meddling), put into its owner's
library (Sudden Setback), airbent (Aang, Swift Savior), or chosen and countered further on (Aether
Spike). Not one of YOUR OWN spells (Goblin Artisans), not one that targets the card itself
(Mistfolk), and not the counterspell a created token carries in its quotes (Mage's Attendant).

Defined 2026-11-08 from Counterspell, "Risposta scelta": the answer is the one you time. Of 10
reviewed cards whose trigger counters a spell by itself as it is cast, only Jin-Gitaxias, Progress
Tyrant carried the tag, and it loses it (named); Invoke Prejudice, Swindler's Scheme and Boromir
(opponents only) stay untagged with the symmetric Nether Void, Void Mirror, Vexing Bauble, Presence
of the Master, In the Eye of Chaos and Bazaar of Wonders. The same reading leaves out the unreviewed
Chalice of the Void, Counterbalance, Lavinia, Erayo and Lunar Force, and the ward-like "becomes the
target …, counter that spell or ability" of Frost Titan or Kira (Parnesse is Protection by hand).
Kadena's Silencer stays: it counters at the moment you turn it face up.

Ruled 2026-11-08, Q1: a counter that protects only the card itself is neither Counter nor Protection
("È protezione per se stessi. Non conta Counter nè Protection"). Mistfolk's "counter target spell
that targets this creature" stays untagged, while Cerulean Drake ("targets you"), Avoid Fate and Ring
of Immortals ("a permanent you control") and Vigilant Martyr ("an enchantment") keep Counter.

Ruled 2026-11-08, Q2: "End the turn" is Counter on an INSTANT only ("Solo istantanei"). Hurkyl's
Final Meditation keeps it, and Time Stop, Discontinuity and Glorious End are proposed; a sorcery
never has their spell on the stack (Ultima, Day's Undoing), and an ability that ends only your own
turn is a combo piece (Obeka, Brute Chronologist; Sundial of the Infinite).

Read 2026-11-08 under the definition: a counter of "up to four" or "up to two" targets (Katara's
Reversal, Double Negative), "exile any number of target spells" (Mindbreak Trap), "counter all
abilities your opponents control" (Kadena's Silencer, Summary Dismissal), and "for each spell and
ability your opponents control, counter it" (Whirlwind Denial).
