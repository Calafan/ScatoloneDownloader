# Wipe

Canonical record of the rulings for `CardEffect.Wipe`. The one-line tooltip shown in the tagger is
in [`EffectGlossary.cs`](../../ScatoloneDownloader/Mtg/EffectGlossary.cs).

mass removal: the board is emptied, by any verb. Destroy, exile, damage, shrink, or return it all to
hand — Desynchronization is a Wrath that happens to give the cards back, and "each player sacrifices
all but one" is a Wrath somebody else performs.

Widened 2026-09-15 to read past the ADJECTIVE, which is where half the sweepers in the game put one:
Anarchy destroys all WHITE permanents, Perish all GREEN creatures, Evaporate damages each WHITE
AND/OR BLUE one. Deliberately excluded: "destroy all creatures blocking or blocked by this creature"
(Abu Ja'far, Kjeldoran Frostbeast, the Glyph cycle), which reads like a sweeper and is a combat
trick.

Widened again 2026-09-27, 73 wrong out of 6,321 reviewed cards down to 25, and none of it was a new
ruling: the sweeper was there and the words around it were one more than the rules allowed.

A TYPE LIST puts a comma before the noun (Jokulhaups, Nevinyrral's Disk); EACH is all by another
word (Wave of Terror, Wrath of the Skies, Selective Obliteration); the shrink needs neither all nor
each ("white creatures get -1/-1", "creatures your opponents control get -1/-1", "other creatures
get -2/-2") and comes singular ("each non-Dragon creature gets -X/-X") or as a -1/-1 COUNTER on each
creature; the damage is "equal to its power", "that much", "divided among all creatures", or lands
on a player "AND EACH CREATURE THAT PLAYER CONTROLS"; airbend all is exile all; and a symmetric
sacrifice of ALL, HALF or A THIRD of the creatures, or of "the rest" of a pile that includes
creatures, is a Wrath the players perform (Living Death, Zodiark, Pox, Balance). Damage to EACH
attacking or EACH blocking creature is a whole side of the combat and stays Wipe (Lava Storm, Hail
Storm). Also excluded, each at 0 tagged among the reviewed cards written that way: only what DIDN'T
ATTACK, was BLOCKED BY that Wall, blocks ENCHANTED creature, or was named as "X target creatures …
each of those creatures" (Season of the Witch, Total War, Coils of the Medusa, Choking Vines, Winter
Blast); a DEXTERITY flip (Chaos Orb, Falling Star); your OWN creatures (Cinder Giant, Vampirism);
creature CARDS in a graveyard (Zombie Mob, Yggdrasil); a pile of LANDS kept (Natural Balance —
LandDestruction's question, not this one); Auras or Equipment ATTACHED to one thing (Scarab of the
Unseen, Blastfire Bolt); a CHOSEN type that can only be artifact or enchantment (Season of Gathering
is Disenchant); and a NAME or a VOTE that picks one thing (Eye of Singularity, Bile Blight,
Council's Judgment, Vault 11). Five rulings the same day, each settling a split in the hand tags.
Damage to EACH CREATURE AND EACH PLAYER is Wipe and Burn, however small and however often — twelve
to seven before, Pestilence against Withering Wisps and Winter Sky against Dry Spell on the same
sentence.

ONE creature each ("each player sacrifices a creature") is an edict and is REMOVAL, not this
(Abyssal Gatekeeper, Wasteland Raider, Season of Loss and Tariff moved over; Accursed Marauder and
Gaius were already there); two or more each, all, or the rest is still a Wrath.

The SAME edict EVERY TURN for every player grinds the board away and IS this (Dystopia, The Abyss,
Nature's Wrath) — a land or an artifact alone is not. Everything printed in one EXPANSION is
(Golgothian Sylex, City in a Bottle, Apocalypse Chime), and so is a permanent sacrificed FOR EACH
permanent of a kind, which is all of them (Omen of Fire, 2026-09-28).

An edict every turn is this tag ALONE (Dystopia lost Removal, 2026-09-28), but one that only ever
lands on the OPPONENTS is Removal however often it comes back (Sothera). And ONE CREATURE TYPE is a
tribal card's business, not a board (Goblin Shrine, Scorch the Fields), read case-sensitively
because the game capitalises a type and never an adjective — "non-Dragon" stays a sweeper.
