# Steal

Canonical record of the rulings for `CardEffect.Steal`. The one-line tooltip shown in the tagger is
in [`EffectGlossary.cs`](../../ScatoloneDownloader/Mtg/EffectGlossary.cs).

threaten / act of treason / control magic (temporary or permanent control theft).

An EXCHANGE is a theft you paid for: Juxtapose, Legerdemain, Political Trickery, Trade the Helm,
Gauntlets of Chaos. Word of Command takes the PLAYER. And a card taken out of somebody else's
LIBRARY, HAND or GRAVEYARD and played is a theft of a card rather than of a permanent — Outrageous
Robbery, Apple of Eden, Laughing Jasper Flint, Rev, Crabomination, Azula. Playing YOUR OWN exiled
card is not: Triple Triad and Wheel of Potential say "cards you own" after a symmetric exile, and an
extra land drop or a second cast for the card itself belong to Ramp and Regrowth.

GIVING A PERMANENT AWAY is the mirror image of this tag and was 12 of its 14 false positives —
Jinxed Idol, Rainbow Vale, Chaos Lord and Stiltzkin pay it as a price, Rohgahh and Emberwilde Djinn
suffer it for missing an upkeep, Guardian Beast negates it. The subject sits immediately in front of
the verb, because "untap target creature AN OPPONENT CONTROLS and gain control of it" (Ray of
Command, Magus of the Unseen) puts the victim in front of a verb that is yours. Reanimating out of
an OPPONENT'S graveyard is both Reanimate and this (Ashen Powder, Bone Dancer, Chorale of the Void,
Fire Nation Salvagers), but ordinary reanimation names no victim — Reanimate, Hymn of Rebirth and
Coffin Queen say "from A graveyard" and stay Reanimate alone.

"THEY may play" counts, but only against a NAMED zone: Gonti exiles from "that OPPONENT'S library"
and hands the card to the creature's controller, while Transforming Flourish has one player exile
from "THEIR library" and then cast it, which is nobody robbing anybody.

A MILL is a zone too, and the only way Locke could ever be found — "each player mills a card … you
may cast a spell from among those cards" names no library at all. Moving somebody else's AURA onto a
permanent of your choosing is theft of its job if not of its control (Enchantment Alteration, Crown
of the Ages). Two shapes were ruled OUT the same day: a token copy of their creature is a body you
made rather than a card you took (Echo Chamber, which is Tokens), and a card pulled at random out of
"that graveyard" names no victim (Mysterious Stranger).

Ruled 2026-09-19; 42 wrong out of 5,037 reviewed cards down to 2, both of them realignments still to
apply.

Read again 2026-10-14 against the sitting's false positives, none of them tagged Steal: the play is
read outside reminder text (an Adventure's "you may cast the creature later", Cruel Somnophage) and
one MODE at a time (Riveteers Charm); an opponent named only as the TRIGGER for playing your own
library is no victim (Virtue of Courage, Fireglass Mentor, Powerbalance), nor is one who exiles
"their library" and plays it themselves (Urabrask); a mill pairs only with YOU playing the card, not
with reanimating from any graveyard (Path of the Schemer, Nezumi Freewheeler); a LAND out of their
graveyard is Ramp (Realmbreaker); "the attacking player" and "the player to your right" are
donations (Contested Game Ball, Hithlain Rope); a TOKEN that steals is the token's (Wake the
Dragon); and moving an Aura YOU control is your own (Codsworth). Steal F1 94.9 to 98.8.
