# Ramp

Canonical record of the rulings for `CardEffect.Ramp`. The one-line tooltip shown in the tagger is
in [`EffectGlossary.cs`](../../ScatoloneDownloader/Mtg/EffectGlossary.cs).

**Definition, 2026-11-09** ("Mana TUO in più"): mana of YOURS beyond the land of the turn, given by
the card: one more land on the battlefield, a source that adds mana (or discounts your OTHER spells)
every turn, or a burst of mana that is the card's point. Rampant Growth is the card. Out as
consequences, not exceptions: a land tapping for one, mana that costs mana (it fixes colour), one
land for one land, mana or lands given to somebody else, and a Treasure or mana riding on another
action. Kept as exceptions with their reasons, ruled below: one Treasure per trigger, a card typed
Treasure, a tribe's discount, and from an attack nothing but several Treasures or mana that stays
the turn (2026-11-12). It held on 801
reviewed cards after the realignment of the same day (FP 0, FN 0; FP 8 and FN 3 before it, each a
card the answers of 2026-11-09 reached).

The rulings below are the history; each one is consistent with the definition or kept as an
exception with its reason.

more mana, or sooner: a mana ability on a creature or rock, a land put onto the battlefield.

A land tapping for its own single mana is just a land — with ONE exception, ruled 2026-09-15: a land
that makes more mana than it costs EVERY turn (Ancient Tomb, Mishra's Workshop; read since
2026-09-29 behind an ability word, Muraganda Raceway, on a station land, Evendo and Uthros, and for
an amount counted by the board, Three Tree City and Nykthos; and since 2026-10-13 X mana of any one
colour counted on a land, Baldur's Gate and The Core, which are ManaFixing too) — but not one that
costs ONE untapped land to enter (Balduvian Trading Post, Soldevi Excavations; Scorched Ruins, two
for four, is) or phases out every other turn (Teferi's Isle), nor a KAROO that hands a land back as
it enters, ruled 2026-09-30 ("togliamo Ramp ai Karoo": Karoo, Coral Atoll, Dormant Volcano,
Everglades, Jungle Basin untagged, Arid Archway already was; the two-colour Ravnica ones are
ManaFixing).

Mana that arrives without a {T} on a permanent you keep is also Ramp, ruled 2026-09-15:
Black Lotus, Lotus Petal and Blood Pet spend THEMSELVES, and Dark Ritual is the same trade written
as a spell. The one exception is a LAND THAT ENTERS TAPPED — Crystal Vein cracks for two and counts,
Dwarven Ruins and Ebon Stronghold do the same thing and do not, because entering tapped costs the
turn the extra mana was meant to buy.

Eight more families ruled 2026-09-18, all of them mana you never had to make or a land you never had
to draw: COST REDUCTION for other spells (the Medallions, the Crystals — but not "this spell costs
{1} less", which was tagged 0 of 40); ANY activated mana ability on a non-land, whatever it charges
(a tap for Llanowar Elves, a counter for Wall of Roots, a card out of hand for Elvish Spirit Guide,
charge counters for the Mana Batteries); EXTRA LAND DROPS; MANA MULTIPLIERS (Mana Flare, High Tide,
Wild Growth); a LAND OUT OF YOUR HAND onto the battlefield; UNTAPPING LANDS; a LAND ONTO THE
BATTLEFIELD by any wording; and TREASURES, but only several at once or made by an ACTIVATED ability
— one Treasure once is a rider, the line already drawn for the Clue, and ONE PER TRIGGER is never
this tag, ruled 2026-09-29 ("un Treasure per trigger mai") after the human took Ramp off Jolene,
Orochi Soul-Reaver, Sword of Wealth and Power and Edward Kenway on 09-23; that overturned "over and
over" and handed back the 27 reviewed cards tagged under it (Magda, Mary Read and Anne Bonny,
Kellogg, Gilded Ghoda …). "A Treasure for each" is several at once on a spell (Covetous Elegy) and
one per trigger on a trigger (Edward Kenway), and the Treasures a removal spell counts by its EXCESS
DAMAGE are its rider (Bottle-Cap Blast, Hell to Pay).

A SAGA CHAPTER's burst of mana is not this tag either, ruled 2026-09-29 (Maximum Carnage, Esper
Origins, Clive's Ifrit and Terra untagged; Summon: G.F. Ifrit and Esper Valigarmanda went back for
review), while a chapter that grants a mana ability for good is read as one (Huatli's Saga).

MANA A PERMANENT ADDS ON A TRIGGER is this tag, read 2026-09-29 (Electro, Rodeo Pyromancers, Muerra,
Eladamri's Vineyard, Karn — 10 of 12 reviewed), but never mana from an ATTACK or combat damage,
ruled the same day ("mai Ramp dall'attacco": Tesak untagged, Brazen Collector and Pygmy Hippo handed
back; lands untapped by an attack too since 2026-11-09, Q6 below), nor a spell's trigger riding on
what the spell does (Mana Drain, Reckless Blaze).

The LAND FACE of a modal double-faced card is a land and its mana is just a land's (9 of 10 reviewed
pay-3-life MDFCs untagged; Witch Enchanter handed back); a front that taps for mana keeps it
(Tangled Florahedron).

A land onto the battlefield is read in three more wordings since 2026-09-29 — dug up in a second
sentence (Clifftop Lookout), a search whose put follows a full stop (Point the Way), a land token
(Overlord of the Hauntwoods) — but a GRAVEYARD's lands returned are not this tag (1 of 8), unless
the card FILLED that graveyard itself, ruled 2026-10-13 (Blossoming Tortoise, Deeproot Wayfinder and
Lumra mill or surveil first; Undergrowth Recon, Altanak and Floral Evoker do not). Nor is mana or a
land drop that waits on something the card cannot do itself, ruled the same day: Jaheira, Friend of
the Forest grants mana to tokens it never makes, Nicanzil puts a land onto the battlefield on
somebody else's explore.

A SPELL's mana is read after a comma or counted before its symbol (Energy Tap, Channel, Spoils of
Evil, Burnt Offering, Metamorphosis), and counts only when it is the spell's point: mana riding on a
kill, a counter, a sweep or a regrowth is not this tag (Narset's Rebuke, Mana Drain, Reckless Blaze,
Sorceress's Schemes — 5 of 5 since The Last Agni Kai lost the tag on 2026-11-09). Against all of
that stands one subtraction, and it is the same ruling read backwards: MANA THAT COSTS MANA adds
none, it converts colour, so Celestial Prism and Fire Sprites are ManaFixing and not Ramp — unless
the trade comes back up, as Astrolabe pays {1} for two. Asked of every mana line on the card, so one
free ability beside a paid one keeps the tag (Mana Prism). Two things the land family does NOT
include, both learned by breaking them: the land handed to the player a removal spell was aimed at
is their consolation, not your ramp (Emergency Eject, Price of Freedom), and ONE land sacrificed for
ONE land is a swap (Renewal) while one for TWO is not (Harrow).

The LANDER token is not in either exclusion and fetches a real land — all 17 reviewed cards that
make one are Ramp.

A POWERSTONE is Ramp however many and however made, one included (11 of 11 reviewed, unlike the
Treasure), read without its reminder since 2026-10-13 (Mishra, Lost to Phyrexia; The Brothers' War;
Hall of Tagsin, a land); not one handed to the controller of what was destroyed (Cityscape Leveler).

Read 2026-10-15: a cost reduction in COLOUR (the Defilers; Eluge's "{U} (or {1})") and of {X}, ruled
the same day ("Ramp tutte": Will, Scion of Peace and Elminster were tagged; Rowan, Scion of War,
Samut, Golden-Tail Trainer and Commander Liara Portyr went back for review); a colour chosen first
and mana handed to "target player" (Radiant Lotus, The Warring Triad, Bigger on the Inside); ten
Treasures (Ancient Adamantoise); mana tripled (Virtue of Strength); a land CREATURE token and the
copies of a target land (Awaken the Woods, Rebuild the City), but not land tokens counted by
commander casts (Jyoti). And NOT: a TRANSFORMED Temple tapping for one (Ojer Axonil, Pakpatiq, Taq;
Aclazotz handed back), though a back face tapping for more stays (The Core, Gold-Forge Garrison); a
Treasure handed to somebody else (An Offer You Can't Refuse); a card TYPED Treasure cracking itself
(Buried Treasure, Courier's Briefcase — the untyped Lotus Petal stays); one Treasure from an ability
that sacrifices the card, ruled the same day ("No Ramp": Volatile Fault, Contested Game Ball;
Pictures of Spider-Man and Reckless Lackey untagged); the land untapped to be animated (Silvanus's
Invoker, Woodcaller Automaton); ONE mana granted to lands, which fixes (Dune Chanter, Wrenn and
Realmbreaker, Spara's Adjudicators — two or three granted is Ramp, 3 of 3); lands sacrificed for the
lands revealed (Hew the Entwood); a land sacrificing a creature for one mana (Lazotep Quarry).

Ruled 2026-10-16: Terra, Magical Adept is not this tag ("Togli Ramp"; it keeps Filter), and a free
cast of a TRIBE is nothing at all (C1, "Sarebbe Cheat ma per le tribali resta niente"): Dracogenesis
loses Ramp, and Charred Foyer // Warped Space keeps only CardAdvantage and Cheat. Read the same day:
a Saga chapter's Treasure for each (Vault 21: House Gambit; written to the chapters after
Cataclysmic Prospecting came up wrong), a land that puts itself from your hand for mana (Talon Gates
of Madara; not one that returns a creature for it, Urban Retreat), mana added after an "if you do"
(Omnath, Locus of All), a tap ability that adds mana a sentence later (Red Death, Shipwrecker) and
lands searched, exiled and put onto the battlefield (Omenpath Journey). Not this: lands untapped
beside "you can't cast spells" (Innocuous Researcher), extra mana spent only on cumulative upkeep
(Snowfall), and the one-mana ability handed to an opponent's creature (Honest Work), to a Moon's
lands (Zhao, the Moon Slayer) or to the land a dead card becomes (Princess Yue). Handed back: Savvy
Trader, Strong Back, Astrolabe, Alchemist's Talent, Boneyard Desecrator and Natural Balance with the
tag; Town Greeter and Dazzling Theater without it; Gift of Estates and Tithe move to ManaFixing.

Ruled 2026-10-24 (the Ramp pass, 696 reviewed: 9 proposed wrongly and 9 missed before, 1 and 6
after, every one of them a ruling below or a known disagreement): a cost reduction for a TRIBE's
spells is not this tag ("No Ramp, è tribale", the free cast of a tribe of 2026-10-16). The human had
untagged Gargos, Vicious Watcher's Hydras and Herald of Slaanesh's Demons; Nogi, Draco-Zealot,
Sarkhan, Soul Aflame, Slinza, the Spiked Stampede, Tombstone, Career Criminal and The Eleventh
Hour's Doctor token, all tagged before that ruling, went back for review. A spell's own subtype is
no tribe (Uncle Iroh's Lesson spells), nor is face-down (Dream Chisel). Mana KEPT from one step to
the next for good is this tag ("Ramp se è standing": Horizon Stone, tagged by hand; Kruphix,
Upwelling, Omnath, Locus of Mana with it; Ashling, Flame Dancer went back for review), but not when
what is kept is mana from attacking ("mai Ramp dall'attacco": Fangorn, Tree Shepherd; Ozai, the
Phoenix King's firebending — overturned 2026-11-12, see the end) nor for the one turn a spell or a
death keeps it (The Last Agni Kai, Su-Chi Cave Guard).

Read the same day — this tag: a spell's MODE that adds mana (Jeska's Will; a one-time mode on a
permanent's trigger is not, Zuko, Conflicted); the lands each opponent searches and puts under your
control (Rootweaver Druid); mana for each player (Yurlok of Scorch Thrash); X mana a sentence after
a charge counter (Empowered Autogenerator); a token with its own mana ability made by an activation,
not named Treasure (Kibo, Uktabi Prince's Bananas); lands searched and then a d20 rolled (Druid of
the Emerald Grove); "until you EXILE a land card" (Venture Forth). Not this tag: the land the owner
of a shuffled permanent finds on top, their consolation (Audacious Swap); one land sacrificed by an
activation for one land, a swap (Elvish Reclaimer); one Treasure from exiling the card from the
graveyard (Halo Scarab) or when its last counter is gone (Reckoner Bankbuster), both riders; the
lands each player short of them may search for after a sweep (Natural Balance, OVERTURNED
2026-11-09, below); "all lands have '{T}: Add one mana of any color'", the one mana granted to
lands, which fixes (Toxicrene); Treasures a TARGET OPPONENT creates, theirs (Vazi, Keen Negotiator,
neither Ramp nor ManaFixing). The known disagreement left that day, The Last Agni Kai, was settled
on 2026-11-09.

Read 2026-10-30: two mana for {2} is mana that costs mana and adds none (Guild Globe, ManaFixing
alone by hand; Terrarion follows), and "untap target creature or land" is not a land untapped for
mana (Civic Gardener; 0 of 8 reviewed with "target permanent"). Not read then (OVERTURNED
2026-11-03, below): Morophon, the Boundless's {W}{U}{B}{R}{G} less for spells of the chosen type,
since a reduction of several symbols was 0 of 1 reviewed (Avatar Aang).

Ruled 2026-11-03 ("Morophon è Ramp"): a cost reduction for OTHER spells written with a RUN of
coloured symbols is the same discount as one symbol, applying the 2026-10-15 "Ramp tutte" coloured
reduction and the 2026-10-30 ruling that a CHOSEN type is no tribe. Morophon, the Boundless's
{W}{U}{B}{R}{G} less for spells of the chosen type, Bard Class's {R}{G} less for legendary spells,
and Avatar Aang's {W}{U}{B}{R}{G} less for every spell. Avatar Aang is the one reviewed card it
reaches (09-11, no Ramp) and goes back for review. A self-discount stays out with several symbols too
("this spell costs {R}{G} less"), and so does a TRIBE's: Edgewalker's "Cleric spells … {W}{B} less"
and Ragemonger's "Minotaur spells … {B}{R} less" are still nothing, the tribal blank reading the run
as well.

Read 2026-11-05: a land put onto the battlefield by any wording is Ramp, now also when the put is
far from the search (Jaheira's Respite, "up to X basic land cards, where X is the number of
creatures attacking you, put those cards onto the battlefield tapped"; Harvest Season and Sylvan
Primordial, unreviewed, the same) and when it sits behind a condition with the hand as the other
branch (Scholar of New Horizons and Stoic Farmer, tagged by hand; Ruin in Their Wake and Boreas
Charger, unreviewed). Embermouth Sentinel's "put that card onto the battlefield tapped instead" is
the fallback of a put on top and stays ManaFixing alone.

Read 2026-11-05 under the 2026-10-30 ruling (Guild Globe): a paid ability that adds as many symbols
as it costs adds none. Jenson Carthalion, Druid Exile's "{5}, {T}: Add {W}{U}{B}{R}{G}" is not Ramp
(reviewed 11-03). Reached by the same wording: Coal Golem (reviewed 09-04, Ramp and ManaFixing by
hand, handed back without Ramp), the five Eggs (Darkwater, Mossfire, Shadowblood, Skycloud,
Sungrass) and Kaleidostone, unreviewed. The abilities that cost less than they add are untouched
(the Signets, Codie, Cormela).

Read 2026-11-05 under the 2026-10-15 "No Ramp" ruling: one Treasure from an ability that DISCARDS
the card itself is a rider, like the sacrifice (Magma Opus, untagged by hand; Creative Outburst and
Elemental Masterpiece, unreviewed, lose the proposal).

Ruled 2026-11-09, six answers under the definition:

- Q1 ("Tenere: mai Ramp"): ONE Treasure per trigger stays out, an exception kept with its reason: a
  Treasure at a time is colour, not quantity. 0 of 28 reviewed cards carried Ramp for it outside an
  attack and 0 of 32 from one, all ManaFixing, while mana ADDED on a trigger is Ramp (20 of 31): the
  pair was Hulking Raptor's "add {G}{G}" each first main phase and Ultimate Green Goblin's Treasure
  each upkeep. Several Treasures from one trigger stay Ramp (Gluntch, Ziatora). No card moved.
- Q2 ("Come i Treasure"): an Eldrazi SPAWN or SCION is read as a Treasure, overturning the reading
  of 2026-09-29 that a token's quoted mana is the token's own (Chittering Dispatcher): several at
  once, or made by an activated ability, are Ramp; one per trigger is not (Basking Broodscale).
  Spawnbed Protector's two Scions at every end step were tagged by hand (11-06). Spawn-Gang
  Commander, Writhing Chrysalis, Drowner of Truth, Kozilek's Command, Kozilek's Unsealing, Idol of
  False Gods and Propagator Drone, reviewed without it (2 of 21 reviewed Spawn cards were Ramp),
  took the tag and went back for review. Path of Annihilation keeps its Ramp for the mana ability
  it grants.
- Q3 ("-Ramp +ManaFixing"): a CREATURE typed Treasure that cracks itself for one mana of any colour
  is the typed Treasure of 2026-10-15 (Buried Treasure): Goldhound loses Ramp and takes ManaFixing.
  The untyped Blood Pet and Lotus Petal stay.
- Q4 ("-Ramp, solo Removal"): The Last Agni Kai's excess damage as {R} rides on the fight, like the
  other kills that add mana; it loses Ramp and keeps Removal.
- Q5 ("+Ramp"): Natural Balance's lands for each player with four or fewer are lands of yours when
  you cast it short of five, symmetric like Veteran Explorer's and Mana Flare's: it takes Ramp and
  keeps LandDestruction, overturning the "Not this tag" of 2026-10-24.
- Q6 ("Solo i Treasure"): from an ATTACK or combat damage only what outlasts the turn counts,
  several Treasures (Prosperous Bandit's "that many", 11 of 11 reviewed; Rapacious One's Spawn).
  Mana and lands UNTAPPED by an attack are spent that turn and are never this tag, which sharpens
  "mai Ramp dall'attacco" of 2026-09-29: Bumi, Unleashed lost Ramp and went back for review; Pip-Boy
  3000's "Untap up to two target lands" mode stays out, and Sword of Feast and Famine, Bear Umbra
  and Nature's Will lose the proposal.

Read the same day: a MODE that adds mana on a REPEATING trigger of a permanent is a source every
turn (Galadriel, Light of Valinor's "Add {G}{G}{G}" as each creature enters, tagged by hand; a
one-time mode stays out, Zuko, Conflicted); the NEXT spell of a tribe costing less is the tribe's
discount of 2026-10-24 (Invasion of the Giants' chapter III, Filter alone by hand).

RULED 2026-11-10 on the 11-09 definition ("+Ramp"): Elemental Teachings, reviewed untagged on
09-10, puts "the rest" of the lands it searched onto the battlefield tapped after the opponent bins
two, which is two lands in play, and is Ramp. Read the same day (OVERTURNED that evening, see
below): a land SUBTYPE that is always a land is the word land in a search ("a Desert card", "a Cave
card", "a Gate card"; 7 of 7 reviewed cards with one are Ramp), so Colossal Rattlewurm, Sand Scout
and Cosmium Confluence, unreviewed, gain it. Maze's End's Gate, fetched by a land that returns
itself, stays a fetch land.

RULED 2026-11-10 evening, from the second sitting ("L'ho tenuto sulle carte che prendono anche
terre base"), OVERTURNING the subtype reading of that morning: a search for a land SUBTYPE ALONE ("a
Desert card", "a Cave card") is not Ramp — Colossal Rattlewurm and Cosmium Confluence untagged in
the sitting, Sand Scout reached and handed back (-Ramp). The subtype BESIDE a land stays Ramp
through the word land: "basic land card or a Desert card" (Dance of the Tumbleweeds, Map the
Frontier, Navigation Orb, Scampering Surveyor, Explore the Underdark: 5 of 5 put onto the
battlefield), "basic land cards and/or Gate cards".

Read the same day: a land out of an OPPONENT'S HAND is not Ramp — Rona, Tolarian Obliterator's "that
source's controller exiles a card from their hand at random. If it's a land card, you may put it
onto the battlefield under your control" is Filter alone by hand, a card of theirs played (Steal.md:
CardAdvantage at most). The same "if it's a land card" off YOUR library stays (8 of 8: Risen Reef,
Nadu, Lantern of Revealing). Oblivion Sower's lands from their LIBRARY are another sentence and stay
proposed, unreviewed.

Read 2026-11-12: a land search whose put onto the battlefield runs past 60 characters (Celebrate the
Harvest's "X is the number of different powers among creatures you control") or says "both cards"
(Sword of Hearth and Home), mana for each colour among your permanents (Faeburrow Elder), and a
tap-and-sacrifice whose mana comes a sentence later (Priest of Forgotten Gods' "… You add {B}{B}").
Out: Treasures an OPPONENT creates (Ingenious Mastery, CardAdvantage alone; not ManaFixing either).
Bloom Tender and Surveyor's Scope follow among the unreviewed.

Ruled 2026-11-12 ("Resta = Ramp"): mana from an ATTACK or combat damage that STAYS is this tag,
superseding "mai Ramp dall'attacco" of 2026-09-29 for that shape. The turn's worth ("Until end of
turn, you don't lose this mana as steps and phases end"): Kessig Naturalist and Klauth, Unrivaled
Ancient, Ramp by hand, Brazen Collector and Tanuki Transplanter handed back with it (2 of 4 reviewed
before the ruling); Photon, Mighty Marvel, Sakiko, Colossal Plow, Grand Warlord Radha and Savage
Ventmaw follow among the unreviewed. Kept for good: Fangorn, Tree Shepherd's "unspent green" and
Ozai, the Phoenix King's firebending turned red, handed back. Out: mana kept only until end of
COMBAT (Avatar Roku, Fire Lord Ozai, 0 of 2) and mana that is lost (Tesak).
